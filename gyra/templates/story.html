Tasks

Subtasks are BINARY. Done or not done — no “in review”, “testing”, or “blocked” states.
  • Must fit in < 4 hours. If bigger, split it.
  • If it needs approval to be finished → it’s not binary. Break it down.
  • Missing a test? That’s its own subtask: yay or nay.
  • Examples: “Add /health endpoint”, “Write unit test for parseDate()”, “Fix typo in README”.
  • Bad examples: “Refactor auth”, “Test the feature”, “Get sign-off from Amy”.
