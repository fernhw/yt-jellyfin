{# ── SECTION STRUCTURE ── #}

Article Structure — Required Tags

Every article must contain these five section tags in order. They are the backbone of the format. Missing tags are added automatically on save — but title= is your responsibility.

[META] title=Your Article Title Here authors=Studio High Ground template=character [HERO] filename.jpg Optional caption line [MAIN_SECTION] # Your Article Title Here The main body of the article goes here. Markdown works fully in this section. [SECONDARY] ## Extra Notes Background detail, lore depth, design notes. [REFERENCES] - Source one - Source two
⚠️ The golden rule: title= in [META] is the only place the title lives. The database reads it from here on every save. If it's blank or missing, the article will not save.
{# ── META FIELDS ── #}

[META] Fields

FieldRequired?Notes
title=YesThe article title. Shown in the infobox, nav, and GDD. Required to save.
authors=RecommendedWho wrote this. Comma-separated.
template=Recommendedcharacter, location, mechanic, generic
status=Optionaldraft, canonical, wip
owner=OptionalTeam owner: design, narrative, art, etc.
any_key=OptionalCustom infobox rows. born=1842 → shows as "Born · 1842" in the sidebar.

{# ── SPOILERS ── #}

Spoiler Tags

||This text is hidden until the reader clicks it.||

Wrap content in double pipes. On the article page it renders as a blurred block the reader must click to reveal.

Upload via the 🖼 Add Image button. The filename is inserted at the cursor. Use 🖼 Set Hero to set the infobox image.
