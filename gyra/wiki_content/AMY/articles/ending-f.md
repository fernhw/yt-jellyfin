# Ending F

Perfect Night (real ending)
Conditions: Give her Baya's best tea; solve 10 story puzzles; collect all of the Alphyds; save the city of quitters; and give Barret the cigar.
Summary: Barret gives Amy the exit keys. Ross himself thanks Amy for everything in a letter given to her, and off Amy goes. Post-credits, we can read it; its an apology and a welcome card. If anyone needs help, she can send them there.
This ending is the feel-good ending; Amy is left to her devices and is forced to leave the hotel. She is always welcome back if she needs help. Always.
Symbolically, Amy closes her heart to the events of her brother's accident but covers them with good deeds.
