[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s hotel was established in the year 1852 by [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) after 4 years of construction in secrecy. The hotel is Gothic/Victorian with a veneer of oddness to it. The whole place has had several modifications during the coming decades, evident from the added rooms, taken holes and severed windows. The hotel is now, in the story, 170 years old and has the same residents it started with, kept here by the [Kubler](the-kubler.md).
