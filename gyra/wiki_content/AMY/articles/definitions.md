# Definitions

A reference glossary of terms, systems, and concepts in *Amy's Escape*. Every name that has a full article is linked. If something is missing here, it belongs here.

---

## World and Lore

**[The Kubler](the-kubler.md)** is the fungal organism grown from a meteor fragment found in [Ross Valley](ross-hotel.md) in 1844. Its spores permeate the hotel and prevent biological death within range. It is the reason nothing and no one inside the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) can die. See also: [Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md), the naturalist who first classified it.

**[God Seeds](god-seeds.md)** are two organic artefacts found in the [Ross Valley](ross-hotel.md) crater. Consuming one rewrites the body's biology permanently and catastrophically. [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) consumed one in 1866. [Wilson Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) consumed one below the mines. There are two on the current game map.

**[Ross Valley](ross-hotel.md)** is the meteor crater in South America purchased by [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) in 1848. It is the origin site of the Kubler organism and the god seeds. Now the location of the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md).

**[The Loops](the-loop.md)** is the in-universe term for the condition affecting certain hotel residents. Immortality applied to a mind that cannot process what is happening results in a person trapped in a single repeating action or state, unable to stop. Multiple loops crystallised simultaneously in 1985 when [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) had his first death.

**[The Lost Children and The Cursed Maids](the-cursed-maids.md)** are two overlapping groups. The Lost Children were terminal cases sent to the hotel by families promised a cure; their organs were used to keep [Jone](jone-ross.md) alive, leaving them hollow shells. The Cursed Maids are the hotel's long-serving domestic staff who never left and were similarly consumed by the hotel over time. [Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md) was a Cursed Maid.

**[Emerette of the Deep](emerette-of-the-deep.md)** is the creature living in the lowest level of the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md). He was Wilson Emerette, a miner. He is what happens when god seed consumption goes as badly as it can.

**[The City of Quitters](city-of-quitters.md)** is a settlement built by the sealed-in miners of [Ingersole](ingersole-caverns.md), maintained with continuous artificial light to keep [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) at bay. Governed by [Relbuk Derfla](relbuk-derfla.md). Home to the miners who refused to leave and were gradually deformed.

**[Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md)** is the Gothic-Victorian hotel built over the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md) in 1852 as cover for [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s research into the Kubler organism.

**[Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md)** are the mines beneath the hotel. Sealed in 1892 after [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) came into being. The setting for Chapters [4](chapter-4.md) and [5](chapter-5.md).

## Characters (Quick Reference)

**[Amy Loevinger](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)** is the protagonist. 10 years old. Her brother [Custard](custard-loevinger.md) died in the hotel. She returned to take the exit key from [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md).

**[Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)** runs the [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md) in the upper hotel. His loop is performing a show with no audience.

**[Baya Ross](baya-ross.md)** is the wife of [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md). Manages the ballroom. Her cooperation unlocks access to the theater.

**[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)** tends the [Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md). Slowly becoming a plant.

**[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md)** is in the attic studio. She has been constructing a dress for 40 years.

**[Nettle Tenesea](nettle-tenesea.md)** is trapped in the hotel's pipe system. She wants to reach open water.

**[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)** is a miner from the original Ingersole crew. His encounter spans the mine hospital and the bar.

**[The Knotts](the-knotts-rat-trap.md)** are a colony of rat-like creatures living in the Rat Trap section.

**[Clementine](clementine-wellington.md) and [Julianne Wellington](julianne-wellington.md)** are a married couple who have not spoken directly in 20 years. They occupy the kitchen and bakery.

**[Dr. Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md)** is a biologist who arrived in 1945 to study the Kubler. She engineered the [moonlight aphids](the-12-moonlight-aphids.md) and [Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md).

**[Relbuk Derfla](relbuk-derfla.md)** governs the [City of Quitters](city-of-quitters.md) as a crow-human hybrid patriarch.

**[Evangeline Aerona](evangeline-aerona-and-ilina.md) and [Ilina](evangeline-aerona-and-ilina.md)** are fortune-teller sisters. Evangeline's loop has made her fluid between floors. Ilina has been reduced to the size of a pea. They are central to [Ending D](ending-d.md).

**[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md)** is the son of [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md). He consumed a god seed and has died and been rebuilt dozens of times. His first death in 1985 triggered the mass loop crystallisation.

**[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)** is the founder of the hotel and the Ingersole operation. The final obstacle.

**[Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)** was the mine's head doctor. Husband of [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md).

**[Martha Waliger](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md)** was the hotel's original cook. She prepared a tea using Kubler-adjacent plants that caused permanent petrifaction. She is now a shrub in the kitchen garden.

## Game Systems

**[Sin System](sin-system.md)** is the game's moral tracking system. Bad solutions to puzzles leave physical marks on [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md): burns, stains, cuts. These marks accumulate visibly and are tied to achievement unlocks. Too many sins affects the endgame encounter with [Jone](jone-ross.md).

**[SYSM - See You See Me](sysm-see-you-see-me-mechanics.md)** is a perspective mechanic. The game is third-person, but when Amy covers her face the camera shows disembodied hands covering the lens from her point of view. The boundary between observer and character collapses at the moment of contact.

**[NOVA Dialog System](the-theater-of-dreams.md)** is the studio's internal dialog tree and screenplay injection tool. Used at its most complex in [Chapter 3](chapter-3.md) for the [Kane](aaron-kane.md) theater sequence, where Amy must navigate conversations with eight talkable puppets.

**[Jackal/Joker](jackaljoker.md)** is the base difficulty mode. Standard puzzles and stealth mechanics.

**[The Loops](the-loop.md)** are covered in detail in their own article and above under World and Lore.

## Locations

**[The Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md)** exists at the threshold of the hotel's reality. Characters who receive good-path resolutions are sent there. Large characters who cannot physically enter receive a [statuette](statuettes-for-larger-characters.md) instead.

**[The Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md)** is [Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)'s theater in the upper hotel, populated by his 40 puppet constructs. Access requires [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md)'s permission or a workaround.

**[The Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md)** is the two-story greenhouse built for [Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md). His domain.

## Collectibles

**[The 12 Moonlight Aphids](the-12-moonlight-aphids.md)** are bioluminescent insects engineered by [Dr. Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) in 1945 as a natural controller for the Kubler. They failed at this function and now wander the hotel. They are the game's primary collectibles.

**[Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md)** is the last surviving firebird, a bioluminescent bird line engineered by [Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) in 1958 to hunt the aphids. The line was made sterile. Darby spent 65 years in a storage cage before [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) found her.

## Meta and ARG

**[Melina's Impossible Puzzle ARG](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md)** is a real-world alternate reality game built around clues left by [Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md), a [Cursed Maid](the-cursed-maids.md) who documented her knowledge in 1985. She addressed every letter with "Well Done Moniker" - a mark of respect for whoever found them. Solving the ARG unlocks an exclusive ending.
