# Logo Design

The logo is the core of branding this was our 9th logo after much work with the design consultants, goal was making it readable in the corner of a page evoke the story of the game, we landed on a logo with custom fonts and serif like text, the text has to have sharp points, create a twisted feeling beaking it's rules becoming something transcendent. It being a text logo meant it needed an isotype. We opted on an adaptive visual logo that can be changed for marketing. Solid meaning its easy on print and billboards.
