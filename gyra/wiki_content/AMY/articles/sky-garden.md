# Sky Garden

The second-floor plant research collection facility, made in the 1850s, is designed of aged-out technology and made primarily to study and raise plants affected by the God Seeds in Ross' search for immortality. By the time the Skygarden was built, Ross had acquired one god seed. The old glass was broken, and the containment breached all the modified plant life, spreading to the fauna. its just a shell of what it once was. Yet Eila keeps it running.
