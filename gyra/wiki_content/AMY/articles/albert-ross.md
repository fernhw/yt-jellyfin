## Overview

Albert Ross founded the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) in 1852. He was already involved with [Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md) by then; the discovery of the crater forest and the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) organism had been made eight years earlier, and Albert was one of the people who understood what it meant. His son [Jone](jone-ross.md) ate a [god-seed](god-seeds.md) in 1866. The hotel's apparent purpose, hospitality, tourism, a respectable establishment in a remote location, was secondary to what Albert actually needed it for: a controlled environment to study and cultivate the [Kubler](the-kubler.md), to keep [Jone](jone-ross.md) alive, and to keep the discovery from becoming public knowledge.

He found immortality. It was a side effect, not a goal. He left in 1978.

## The Departure

Albert left the hotel because he became afraid, not of what he had built, but of what people would do with it if they found it. He was afraid specifically of people who would try to help him, which is a particular kind of fear that only develops after a very long time of watching what help looks like at scale. He has not been seen in the game's events. He is alive. He is somewhere. He is "Mr. Ross."

This is a distinction the game maintains carefully: every reference to "Mr. Ross" in the hotel refers to [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md), Albert's son. Albert is never referenced as Mr. Ross. He is Albert, or he is absent.

## Relationship with the Hotel

Albert's friendship with [Kubler](alfred-kubler.md) was the genuine constant of his life. His relationship with [Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md) was one of absolute trust given and absolutely kept; [Barret](barret-aricson.md) has held the hotel together for decades since Albert left, without being asked again and without expecting Albert to return. His relationship with [Baya](baya-ross.md), his wife, is complex in ways the hotel makes legible slowly.

Albert Ross never appears in *Amy's Escape.* His presence is felt through every room.
