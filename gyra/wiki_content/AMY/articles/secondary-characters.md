# Secondary characters

Dr. Elizabeth Blueth Needham - A woman of simple needs, Dr. Blueth enjoys playing solitaire and drinking tea. She is the proud caretaker of 543 insects and has remained solitary for an extended period, causing her playing cards to lose their color.
Francena Cadwallader - A renowned dress designer and wife of Dr. Daniel Cadwallader, Francena was given a personal design studio at the Ross Hotel as a condition of her relocation. Despite having many fans in the past, she has not released a new design in 80 years, causing most of her followers to pass away.
Clementine Wellington - A small, loud, and angry woman, Clementine runs the hotel's kitchen. Her skills were honed through worldwide travels before settling at the prestigious Ross Hotel.
Julianne Wellington - Clementine's loving husband, remains puzzled by the cause of his wife's anger and patiently awaits her forgiveness so they can be together again.
Raeburn “Rae” Chastel - A large bartender who speaks in an odd manner, Rae is tight-lipped about his past and doesn't engage in much conversation.
Jenna Martina - A regular at the hotel bar, Jenna spends her days drinking from opening to closing time, making her the establishment's best customer.
Evangeline Aerona and Ilina - A pair of fortune-telling sisters living on the first floor. Ilina is the one who sees possible futures; too bad she is the size of a large pea and needs her sister to talk up for her.
Albert Ross - is the founder of the Albert Ross Hotel. He created the place as a retirement home and hasn't left it ever since.
Jone Ross - is the son of Baya and Albert Ross.
Alfred Kubler - scientist who found the flowers in the ingersole Mines.
Custard Loevinger - is Amy’s brother.
Sam Bernal - is Sam Bernal, is the guy who is sleeping near to the duster.
The Cursed Maids - are a group of maids and servants who have worked at the hotel and are rumored to have met a terrible fate. Cursed Maid #1 is the 5th maid of the Albert Ross Hotel, Melina Lotus (Cursed Maid) #2 Broken Maid #4 is the 3rd maid of the hotel, and Cursed Maid #5 is the 6th maid of the hotel.
Martha Gioseppy Waliger - is the former housekeeper of the Albert Ross Hotel. She turned into a bush, and no one knows how it happened. Martha was a tea enthusiast, so much so that she carried a tea diary wherever she went. The teas she made were the hotel owner's favorites.
Sara Reizes Klein - is a girl who lives in the walls with long white hair. She's supposed to be the only person to ever escape but never did. You meet her in a secret place.
Emerette of the deep - The first immortal, walks towards you in the ingersole Mines trying to catch Amy.
