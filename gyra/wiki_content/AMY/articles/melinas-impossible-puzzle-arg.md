## Melina

Melina was a maid at the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md), one of the [Cursed Maids](the-cursed-maids.md), and the first to seriously attempt escape. The attempt cost her her legs. The hotel's [Kubler](the-kubler.md) field kept her alive regardless. She stopped trying to run after that, but she did not stop thinking.

In 1985, using a computer available to her during the chaos following [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md)'s death and the multi-loop activation at Jenna's party, Melina documented what she knew: the hotel's structure, its secrets, the way the loops worked, what the god-seeds were. She addressed every note and letter with "Well Done Moniker", a deliberate, personal acknowledgment for whoever eventually found the records. An assumption that someone would. A hope.

She eventually succumbed to the hotel in the same way all the maids did. The records survived her.

## The ARG

Melina's records extend outside the game. Clues within *Amy's Escape* point to real-world locations, websites, encoded documents, community puzzles, that collectively reconstruct what Melina knew. The design is inspired by the Demon's Souls Remake medallion ARG: layered, community-driven, requiring collective effort to resolve.

Completing the ARG unlocks an ending that cannot be reached through in-game play alone.
