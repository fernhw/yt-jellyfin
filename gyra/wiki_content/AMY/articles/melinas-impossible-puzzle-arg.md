Melina was a maid in the Ross Hotel who never left, in an attempt to run away lost her legs and with it her will to live, she left an impossible puzzle for new maids. She starts every letter with "Well Done Moniker" to give a sense of accomplishment.
Melina's ARG (Alternate Reality Game) is designed to engage players beyond the confines of Amy's Escape, providing a multi-layered experience that drives conversation, fosters community, and rewards dedicated players with unique insights and an exclusive ending. Inspired by the Demon's Souls Remake ARG with the medallions, this ARG will push the boundaries of in-game storytelling and player interaction.
