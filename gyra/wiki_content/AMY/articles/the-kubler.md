[MAIN_SECTION]
A white flower-like fungal creature discovered by Alfred Kubler, the Kubler plant is a singular fungal life form appearing as a white-flowered field. It is present all around the game. Its spores keep the Ross Hotel alive and well. Fungal structures are but one entity; the Ross Hotel has two fighting Kubler megaorganisms.
1.3.1 The Lost Ones
Children with terminal illnesses were sent to the Ross Hotel at the request of their parents. They work in the kitchen under Clementine's rule.
1.3.1 Sky Garden
The second-floor plant research collection facility, made in the 1850s, is designed of aged-out technology and made primarily to study and raise plants affected by the God Seeds in Ross' search for immortality. By the time the Skygarden was built, Ross had acquired one god seed. The old glass was broken, and the containment breached all the modified plant life, spreading to the fauna. its just a shell of what it once was. Yet Eila keeps it running.
1.3.1 Marionette Audience
Kane, once the main engineer of Ross' army of scientists, reclused himself in the theater to enjoy his acting, eventually bored and creating a marionette. Then, more and more, eventually understanding the curse, he found a way to take off parts of himself (tendons, muscles, etc.) to create puppets controlled by him. Eventually, there were 40 bodies, similar to a fungal creature, with one person controlling several.
1.3.1 The God seeds
also named Tear of God and Immortality Seed, These metaphysical miracles found in the ingersole mines—they're the fantastical element that drives the story; harnessing or curing these breathing organisms creates the kubler, or even considered the source of the kubler. But as any organism it needs to eat is found to be very adaptable, the kubler will figure out how to survive by all means necessary.
