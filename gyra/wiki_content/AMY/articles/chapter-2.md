## Overview

Chapter 2, *Thorn*, corresponds to Anger in the [Kubler-Ross grief model](world-timeline.md). [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)'s path takes her down through the hotel from the fifth floor to the first basement level, where the entrance to [Kane's theater](the-theater-of-dreams.md) is located. The name *Thorn* refers to the sensation of being torn apart, the grief stage's specific texture, and the structural texture of the chapter itself, which places Amy between people with irreconcilable positions.

## Path Through the Chapter

[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) descends through the hotel ballroom and into the kitchen. She encounters [Esther Persson](the-cursed-maids.md), the Hollow Maid, early in the chapter. Esther is an ally, provided Amy understands her correctly; she uses a knife to communicate, she is missing certain face parts, and she has a specific goal involving the top floor of the hotel that Amy can either help or hinder.

The kitchen belongs to [Clementine Wellington](clementine-wellington.md). Getting through it requires navigating a marriage that has been in active quarrel for generations: [Julianne](julianne-wellington.md), nine metres tall, scared, just down the hall, and [Clementine](clementine-wellington.md), who hears only what she wants to hear. Both are technically correct about different things. Amy needs to pass through, not fix anything.

## The Baya Puzzle

[Baya Ross](baya-ross.md) is the chapter's central puzzle and its most significant character. She is twelve metres tall, the hotel matriarch, and she is hungry. Amy must find a way to deal with this to proceed to the theater entrance.

Three solutions exist:
1. **Good**: Find Baya's favourite tea blend (Flories) in [Martha Waliger](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md)'s Tea Book in Martha's room. Baya receives it with joy. She tells Amy what she knows about [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) and grants access to the theater, which is adults-only and requires her authorisation.
2. **Neutral**: Administer a common sleepy tea. Baya sleeps. Passage is possible. Less is gained.
3. **Bad (sin)**: The stone tea recipe from [Martha](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md)'s Tea Book, the same recipe that turned [Martha](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md) into a shrub. Applied to [Baya](baya-ross.md), it turns her into a very large plant. The [Sin System](sin-system.md) registers this.

## Eila Bartlett

[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md) is Chapter 2's anger antagonist in the purest sense. He maintains the hotel [garden](the-sky-garden.md) and will not allow [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) to carry two plants at once. Taking a second plant triggers his game-over. He is not interested in reasons.

## Chapter End

The chapter ends when [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) reaches the first basement entrance to [Kane's theater](the-theater-of-dreams.md). She does not enter the theater in Chapter 2. She earns the right to enter.
