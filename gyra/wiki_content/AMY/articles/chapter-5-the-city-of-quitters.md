# Chapter 5: "The City Of Quitters"

Amy navigates the treacherous Ingersole Caverns and discovers the City of Quitters, home to transformed miners and their crow-person patriarch, Rulbek. As she overcomes challenges and puzzles, she gains allies for her impending confrontation with the monster in the mines, Emerette.
