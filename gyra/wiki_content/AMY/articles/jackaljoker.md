[SECONDARY]
## Overview

Jackal/Joker is the name given to the default difficulty setting. It is not a character, not a location, and not a narrative concept — it is a label for the experience the game was designed around: puzzles with full complexity, stealth sequences at intended tension, and the [Sin System](sin-system.md) operating as written.

The name's origin is not explained in-game. The pairing of two card-game terms for a mode that is neither high nor low stakes is presumably deliberate.

## What It Means in Practice

In Jackal/Joker, all three solutions to each story puzzle are available and equally viable. Stealth encounters with [Barret](barret-aricson.md) and others operate at the standard noise and detection thresholds. The NOVA dialog system in [Chapter 3](chapter-3.md) does not simplify. The [Sin System](sin-system.md) tracks everything.
