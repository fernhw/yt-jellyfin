# In Case Of Exclusivity Acquisition By Microsoft

Nintendo, or Sony
In the event of an exclusivity acquisition offer from a platform such as Epic Games, Steam, Sony, or Microsoft, we have outlined a clear exit strategy for investors. If the acquisition offer is $4 million USD or more, we will use this amount to repay the initial investment made by our investors, cover any extra expenses, and distribute the remaining profits according to the agreed-upon revenue-sharing plan.
Offers below 4 million will be discussed, we will discuss it as it is a return on investment, and free ads/publicity, we have to consider all deals.
The decision to accept an exclusivity acquisition will not affect the potential for further sales after the exclusivity period ends. Our projections indicate that an immediate $4 million payout is a viable option, considering the potential long-term benefits of such a deal.
It is important to note that console and PC exclusive deals are separate. A game exclusive to a console like PlayStation may still have the option to be sold on PC platforms, thus not eliminating all potential sales channels. This approach ensures that our investors continue to benefit from the game's success, even with an exclusive agreement in place.
By outlining this exit strategy, we aim to provide a transparent plan for investors, ensuring that their interests are protected, and they can expect a favorable return on investment should an exclusivity acquisition occur.
