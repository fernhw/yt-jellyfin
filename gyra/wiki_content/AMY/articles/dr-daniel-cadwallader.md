## Overview
Dr. Cadwallader is among the most visually striking figures in the hotel. His head hangs from the [attic](attic.md) ceiling on a neck of impossible length, pale, still, too long to have grown naturally. He has no visible eyes or mouth.

His body is in the dressing room adjacent to [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md)'s tailoring studio, both rooms are in the [attic](attic.md). His neck connects them, snaking through the space between. He cannot go anywhere. But when he decides to help [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md), he peels a poster from the attic wall in a slow, resigned motion, revealing a door. Behind that door is Francena's studio. Amy has to go through it to reach him.

## Origin
[Dr. Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md) was brought to the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) as a physician, hired specifically to treat [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md)' severe illness. He refused at first. Financial incentives changed that. He relocated. He became aware of the [God Seed](god-seeds.md) experiments in the [Ingersole Mines](ingersole-caverns.md). He participated.

What he did down there is not disclosed in [Chapter 1](chapter-1.md). It is implied by his transformation, by the location of his neck's anchor point (inside [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md)'s tailoring room, on the [attic](attic.md) level above the mines), and by the specific urgency with which he sends [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) toward the elevator. He is correcting something. He knows what she will find below.

## Communication
Cadwallader cannot speak. He has no mouth. He communicates through a precise vocabulary of physical signals: nods, head shakes, tilts, pauses, and through writing. His neck slithers his hands to wherever [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) is standing in the attic, and he writes. His notes are spare. He does not over-explain. The act of his hands arriving near her, detached from any visible body, carrying a message, is its own form of communication.

[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) learns to read him. Their negotiation in [Chapter 1](chapter-1.md), two people working out a language between them without a word spoken, is one of the chapter's quietest sequences.

## Francena
His wife, **[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md)**, works in the tailoring studio directly next to the dressing room where his body sits. She is making a dress. She has been making it for eighty years. They are separated by a wall and an uncrossable distance of their own making, physically adjacent, effectively unreachable. His neck passes through the space between. He has not gone through the door himself.

---

## The Puzzle — Through Francena's Studio
Cadwallader offers to help [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md). To reach him, she must get to his body in the dressing room, and the only path there is through [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md)'s tailoring studio. Cadwallader removes the poster. The door is there. Amy goes through.

What happens in the studio — and how Amy treats Francena and the dress — determines what Cadwallader can offer at the end of it. He cannot finish anything himself. He has not been able to for a very long time. Amy passing through and doing what he cannot is the whole shape of his puzzle.
