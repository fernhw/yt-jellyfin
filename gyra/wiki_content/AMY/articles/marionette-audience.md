# Marionette Audience

Kane, once the main engineer of Ross' army of scientists, reclused himself in the theater to enjoy his acting, eventually bored and creating a marionette. Then, more and more, eventually understanding the curse, he found a way to take off parts of himself (tendons, muscles, etc.) to create puppets controlled by him. Eventually, there were 40 bodies, similar to a fungal creature, with one person controlling several.
