# Distribution Channels

Amy's Escape will be available for digital download on platforms such as Steam, GOG, and the Epic Games Store. Console versions will be released on PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch. Physical copies of the game, including collector's editions with bonus content and merchandise, will be available through select retailers and the game's official website.
