## Overview

Clementine Wellington is the hotel cook and [Julianne Wellington](julianne-wellington.md)'s wife. She's been in that kitchen for most of the hotel's existence. It's her workspace and her home.

Her marriage to [Julianne](julianne-wellington.md) is long, loud, and ongoing. She hears what she wants to hear. He hears what he wants to hear. The quarrel between them has been running for so long that it has become part of the hotel's ambient noise, like the pipes or the smell of the [Kubler](the-kubler.md)'s flowers.

## In Gameplay

[Clementine's Kitchen](clementines-kitchen.md) is accessible from [Chapter 1](chapter-1.md); nothing physically prevents [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) from reaching it early. It becomes more relevant in [Chapter 2](chapter-2.md), where the kitchen is part of Amy's path downward through the hotel. Clementine herself is an active presence throughout Chapter 2 in the way that large people in small kitchens tend to be: unavoidable.

The lover's quarrel between Clementine and [Julianne](julianne-wellington.md) is a puzzle of its own. Both are technically correct about different things and neither is listening. [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)'s job is to navigate, not mediate, although the distinction blurs.

## The Name

Clementine is a small orange, which is either thematically meaningful or a coincidence, depending on how much credit you give the naming choices in this game. The kitchen is warm. She is not, as a rule, warm toward people who enter it uninvited.
