## Overview

Chapter 3, *Graft*, corresponds to Bargaining in the [Kubler-Ross grief model](world-timeline.md). [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) enters [Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)'s theater in the upper hotel, navigates forty puppets, and finds a way through. Everything in this chapter is a transaction: information for passage, payment for silence, performance for outcome.

## The Theater

Kane's theater is in the upper floors of the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md). It is populated by approximately forty [puppet constructs](the-puppets.md) across which Kane has distributed his consciousness and identity. Eight of these puppets can hold a conversation. The NOVA dialog system, the game's most complex conversation mechanic introduced here, governs these interactions. [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) can: gather information from the eight talkable puppets and use it to convince them to collectively eject Kane from his own theater; pay her way through; or move through the space invisibly without being seen.

Kane does not want to fight Amy. He wants to be left alone. He has been alone in his own loop for a long time and built the theater because building something was better than not building something. Amy is a complication he would prefer to resolve without incident.

## Julianne and the Chapter Junction

[Julianne Wellington](julianne-wellington.md) occupies the space between [Chapter 2](chapter-2.md) and [Chapter 3](chapter-3.md), a door and a hallway from the theater entrance. He maps to both Anger and Bargaining symbolically, which is why his placement is at the junction. He is a man too big for his space, scared, negotiating with a marriage that will not resolve. He reflects the chapter's movement from uncontrolled anger into the kind of anger that tries to negotiate itself down to something manageable.

## Kane's Fate

Regardless of how Amy navigates the chapter, Kane falls forty stories at its conclusion. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) organism prevents him from dying. He comes apart instead. In the good ending route for his arc, the [Ingersole miners](city-of-quitters.md) find his distributed puppet constructs and embrace what he built as art. He wanted to be alone. He is found instead. The game does not tell you if he considers this better.
