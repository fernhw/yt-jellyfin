[MAIN_SECTION]
The events of *Amy's Escape* span 181 years — from a university research expedition in South America in 1844 to a present-day hotel in which nothing has moved toward an exit in forty years. The game itself takes place entirely in the final hours of that stillness.

[SECONDARY]
> **Reading note:** Characters under the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) immortality effect do not age normally after sufficient exposure. Visual age diverges from chronological age over time. Where both are known, both are listed.

## ERA I — Before the Hotel (pre-1848)

### 1779
**[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)** is born. He will become the foremost plant biologist of his era, teaching at university for decades before his field moves on without him.

### 1820
**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)** is born. He will study under Eila and become a field researcher in botany and biology.

### 1822
**[Dr. Elizabeth Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md)** is born.

### 1825
**[Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)** is born. He will become one of the foremost mechanical engineers of his generation.

### 1828
**[Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md)** is born. He will become a geneticist — among the best in his time.

### 1829
**[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)** is born into a wealthy oil family. He has a degree, ambition, and money, and he will spend his life trying to make all three mean something.

### 1830
**[Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md)** is born.

### 1834
**[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)** is born. He grows up poor.

### 1835
**[Martha Gioseppy Waliger](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md)** and **[Baya](baya-ross.md)** are born, approximately.

### 1840
**[Raeburn Chastel](raeburn-rae-chastel.md)** is born.

### 1842
**[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)** retires from university at approximately 63. His field has moved past him. His family is gone. He has no garden. He is already the oldest person connected to what will become this project.

He does not stop working. He cannot. This is not ambition — it is the only available alternative to feeling what his life has cost him. He will spend the next decade looking for somewhere to put it. He does not know that the Kubler is already the answer. He does not know that the Kubler does not need to give him a loop — he already has one. It will only need to make it permanent.

### 1844
**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)**, age 24, on a university field research expedition in South America, discovers a meteor crater — later purchased and renamed **Ross Valley**. The meteoric material contains organisms that cannot be killed. Tissue samples regenerate under lethal conditions. Nothing in the existing taxonomy explains it. Kubler publishes. The paper does not travel far in mainstream channels, but a summary of it reaches, through a chain of acquaintances, a 15-year-old from a wealthy oil family who is still in school.

### 1846
**[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)**, age 17, leaves university — the same institution where [Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md) taught both him and [Kubler](alfred-kubler.md). He has spent a year absorbing everything published about Ross Valley. He has a plan.

That same year, **[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)**, age 12, becomes Ross's personal assistant. A poor kid, brought into the orbit of old money. It is the beginning of something that will consume the rest of his life.

## ERA II — Construction and Assembly (1848–1860s)

### 1848
Ross, age 19, acquires Ross Valley outright. The transaction is recorded as a private land purchase for development purposes. The scientific content is not disclosed in the public record. An unnamed wealthy private investor contributes **$12 million** to the project — the capital that makes construction possible. This person dies shortly after. Their money remains.

[Kubler](alfred-kubler.md) is retained as lead researcher. Hotel construction begins.

### 1852
The **[Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md)** opens after four years of deliberate, secretive construction. Gothic-Victorian in form. Ornate enough that the mine shaft directly beneath it is invisible from the outside.

[Ross](albert-ross.md) is 23. [Barret](barret-aricson.md) is 18. [Kane](aaron-kane.md) is 27, installed the VIP elevator shaft running from the upper floors all the way down into what will become the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md) — based on the Otis steam-driven design, heavily modified for depth.

The scientific cohort assembles over the following years, staggered as the research requires specialists:

- [Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md), 73 — botanist, the oldest in the building before the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) even takes root in the walls. Given the [Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md): a two-story greenhouse purpose-built for him.
- [Kubler](alfred-kubler.md), 32 — lead researcher, operating from the [Ingersole](ingersole-caverns.md)
- [Dr. Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md), 30 — entomologist and biological countermeasures
- [Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md), 24 — geneticist; rivals with Blueth from their first meeting
- [Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md), 22 — lead manager; holds the key to the basement lock
- [Martha Gioseppy Waliger](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md), 17 — head of housekeeping; the only person permitted to harvest from Eila's garden, because she is the only one [Baya](baya-ross.md) trusts to make her teas
- [Raeburn Chastel](raeburn-rae-chastel.md), 12 — bartender; too tall for most rooms from a very young age

### 1854
[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md), age 25, marries **[Baya](baya-ross.md)**. She falls ill shortly after — lovesickness, or something that looks like it.

### 1855
[Ross](albert-ross.md) recruits the finest physician he can find: **[Dr. Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)**, age 40, who refuses twice before the financial offer becomes impossible to decline. The condition of his arrival: his wife, fashion designer **[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md)**, age 35, must have a dedicated studio. A tailoring room is built for her in the [Attic](attic.md). They arrive together.

Francena designs the hotel's operational uniforms. Daniel treats Baya. For a time, they are two people in a functioning marriage inside a strange and isolated place.

Baya recovers. The accounts describe it as a miracle. It is not entirely a miracle.

### 1857
**[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md)** is born. [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) is 28.

### 1866
[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md), age 9, consumes a [God Seed](god-seeds.md). Access to the seed samples was insufficiently restricted. The transformation is not immediate and not clean. The God Seed becomes part of his biology — and his biology becomes something that requires continuous external maintenance to survive. He does not age outward from this point. He remains, visually, a 9-year-old boy. He is also 168 years old by the time [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) arrives.

[Ross](albert-ross.md), confronted with what his ambition produced, accelerates the research rather than stops it. He is looking for a cure.

## ERA III — The Experiments and Their Consequences (1870s–1893)

### ~1870–1890
The [God Seeds](god-seeds.md) — also catalogued by [Kubler](alfred-kubler.md) as *Tears of God* and *Immortality Seeds* — are studied intensively. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) plant organism, grown from the seeds' ancient spores embedded in the meteor, has by now spread through the hotel's structure. Its key property has become undeniable: the spore field prevents the biological death of organisms within sufficient proximity to it. The mechanism is symbiotic. The Kubler requires living hosts. It keeps them alive to maintain this.

The ethical implications are clear. The cohort proceeds.

### 1892
An experiment in the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md) produces an outcome no one planned for. **[Emerette of the Deep](emerette-of-the-deep.md)** — a ten-meter creature, the first fully immortal organism to emerge from God Seed exposure — comes into being.

**[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)**, age 58, attempts to seal the mines before Emerette can reach the hotel floors. Emerette crushes his head during the sealing. Barret dies. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) revives him. He is no longer who he was — not the sweet Barry his friends remember. The drapes he wears over his face from this point are not decorative.

**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)**, age 72, is caught in the crossfire. He does not emerge. Over subsequent years, in darkness, in the deep Kubler spore field, alongside the miners who were also sealed in, he transforms. What he becomes walks among the [City of Quitters](city-of-quitters.md) as its crow-person patriarch: **[Relbuk Derfla](relbuk-derfla.md)**. His name reversed. His shape unrecognisable. He believed he could fly out. He could not.

### 1893
[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md), age 64, having seen the full result of his project, issues the order: **no one leaves.** [Barret](barret-aricson.md)'s standing mission from this point: maintain the hotel, keep the inhabitants inside, keep [Jone](jone-ross.md) alive by whatever means available, and wait for correspondence from Ross.

The **Lost Children** program begins through a private foundation established by Ross — terminal cases sent by desperate parents to a place that promises life. The arrangement is that the children live. What it costs them to live is not in the agreement. [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md), whose God Seed-altered body requires transplanted tissue to persist, is kept alive through this system. Adult tissue degrades in him quickly. The children's does not.

## ERA IV — The Long Silence (1900s–1985)

### ~1920
**[Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)**, long since retired from engineering, begins building his first marionette puppet from materials at hand. Then more. Then — understanding the curse and the nature of what the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) has made of him, and finding that his engineering work is done — he retreats into what he always actually was: a man who wanted to make theater.

He begins distributing parts of his own nervous system outward into his puppet bodies. Each one he can feel, control, direct. The [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md) is built not as a venue but as the only context in which all of him is in the same room at once. The shows are terrible — he knows this. Every piece of himself watching from the seats hates what the piece of himself on stage is doing, including the one still calling himself Kane. He does this anyway. He cannot stop.

By 2025 there are 40 distributed bodies. The theater has not been dark in over a century. At intervals, Kane throws himself into the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md). The fall does nothing. The Kubler reassembles him. The show continues.

### 1945
**[Dr. Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md)**, age 123, engineers the **[Moonlight Aphids](the-12-moonlight-aphids.md)** — bioluminescent insects designed to feed on and suppress the [Kubler](the-kubler.md). They ignore the Kubler entirely and wander the hotel indefinitely. They become its most pervasive residents. They are still here.

### 1958
Blueth, age 136, engineers a second countermeasure: the **Firebirds** — a line of bioluminescent birds designed to hunt the aphids. They also fail to perform their function. Blueth makes the entire line sterile to prevent further spread.

### 1960
The last surviving Firebird is caged in a storage room. Blueth forgets about it. [Barret](barret-aricson.md) forgets about it. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) keeps it alive regardless. It waits in darkness for **65 years.**

### 1978
**[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)**, age 149 — the Kubler has kept him alive far beyond any natural span — leaves the hotel. He tells Barret he is going to make things right and that he will return. This is his second-to-last communication.

### 1985
**[Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md)**'s birthday. Age 155. The guests gather at the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md). There is something resembling a party. No one can fully articulate afterwards why everyone went.

That night, **[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md)** has his first death — the God Seed inside him falters. His body begins to fail in ways previous maintenance cannot address. [Barret](barret-aricson.md) performs emergency surgery.

The Kubler responds to Jone's death with a surge of compensatory biological activity that propagates through the entire hotel. **Most of the residents' behavioural loops crystallise on this night.** The hotel stops being a place people live in. It becomes a machine that lives in them. [Jenna](jenna-martina.md) will celebrate this birthday forever. [Sam](sam-bernal.md) will not wake. [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md) will not finish the dress. [Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) will play the same unwinnable hand. None of them know.

[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) writes his **last letter** to Barret. He says he is coming back.

**[Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md)**, one of the [Cursed Maids](the-cursed-maids.md), uses a computer to document what she knows. The record survives. Finding it is the ARG.

Ross does not come back.

## ERA V — Forty Years of Nothing (1985–2025)

### 1985–2012
The [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) is, in every practical sense, frozen. No new arrivals through official channels. No departures. [Barret](barret-aricson.md) maintains the premises. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) continues. [Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md), age 206 in 1985, is by this point more tree than man. [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md) has not finished the dress. [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s neck is longer than it was. [Sam](sam-bernal.md) cannot wake up. Something outside the hotel still sends the bar's supplies in. Nobody asks why.

### 2012
**[Kole Wadjet](kole-wadjet.md)** is hired by [Barret](barret-aricson.md). Age 42. He arrives under NDA, takes the reception desk, and begins the work of being second to everything. He has been here 13 years by the time [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) arrives.

### 2025
**[Custard Loevinger](custard-loevinger.md)**, age 8, drinks cleaning supplies while [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) is nearby — doing homework, in her own head, being fourteen. She realises. She tries to help. She cannot. He dies.

**[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)** runs. Not because she made a clearly wrong choice but because she was present, normal, and it happened anyway. Weeks later — somewhere lost, somewhere not the hotel — she attempts to end her life.

The [Kubler](the-kubler.md)'s immortality field does not distinguish between a child swallowed by the building's hunger and a girl dying in its proximity. It intercepts her. **[Amelia "Amy" Loevinger](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)**, age 14, is reassembled. She wakes in the [Ventilation Maintenance Room](ventilation-maintenance-room-amys-room.md) with a set of keys she doesn't remember picking up. Scars on her that the hotel put there, not her. Her iPhone is in her jacket pocket. No signal.

[Barret](barret-aricson.md) does not yet know the keys are missing.

**The game begins.**

## Quick Reference — Character Loops

The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) does not merely keep people alive. Over sufficient exposure it locks them into **behavioural loops** — compulsive repetitions of the need that most defined them when their transformation deepened. Characters cannot perceive their own loops from the inside. To them it feels like a choice, a preference, a normal day. The loops are not dramatic: eating, tending plants, cooking, making something for a party that never comes. They are an exact mirror of the things we already do — the Kubler does not invent new compulsions, it simply makes the ones already there permanent.

**Most loops crystallised in 1985**, when [Jone](jone-ross.md) died for the first time and the Kubler flooded the hotel with a surge of compensatory biological activity. This is the night the hotel stopped being a place people lived in and became a machine that lived in them.

**Eila's loop is the first.** It did not start in the hotel. It did not start at his retirement. It was always in him — the man who could not stop working when the world moved past him, who poured himself into his field until his field no longer needed him, who came to the hotel at 73 and was given a garden and never left it. The Kubler did not create Eila's loop. It gave his avoidance somewhere permanent to go. His loop is not circular — it is directional. Each iteration he is less what he was and more what he studied. He is not replaying a moment. He is being consumed by a trajectory. He was the first resident the Kubler understood completely.

**Baya's loop is the second exception.** It began in the mid-1850s — around the time of her illness, her recovery, and whatever [Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md) actually did to cure her. She has been looping for approximately **170 years**. Nobody in the hotel can tell. Her transformation is so complete that there is no baseline left to compare her against. She is not recognisably looping because she is not recognisably anything she once was.

**Kane's loop did not come from Jone's death.** It came later — after his engineering duties were done, after he had nothing left to build. He retreated into his truest passion. He built the [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md). He made puppets. He distributed parts of his own nervous system outward into each puppet body until he had an ensemble — an audience of himself, watching shows he wrote, performed by pieces of himself he detached and directed. The shows are terrible. He knows this. Every part of himself hates the work, including the part still calling himself Kane. Eventually even this is not enough. He throws himself from the theater into the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md) — a long drop that does exactly nothing. The Kubler puts him back in pieces. The show continues.

## ERA I — Before the Hotel
### The Crater and the Meteor
*~Early-to-mid 1800s*

In a remote crater in South America — later purchased outright and renamed **Ross Valley** — a university research team discovers an anomaly in the rock: organisms embedded in meteoric material that cannot be killed. Tissue samples exposed to lethal conditions regenerate. Nothing in the existing taxonomy explains it.

The lead researcher is **[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)**, a botanist and biologist. His professor and longtime mentor is **[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)**, already retired, already one of the foremost plant scientists of his era. Eila does not join the expedition. He is, by this point, old enough that most people assume he will not be doing much of anything anymore.

Kubler reports the find through academic channels. The paper does not travel far. But a summary of it reaches, through a chain of acquaintances, a young man from a wealthy oil family who has a degree, some ambition, and the financial capacity to act on both.

His name is **[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)**.

---

### Ross Acquires Ross Valley
*~1848*

Ross buys the entire lot. The price is significant. The acquisition is conducted quietly, recorded as a land purchase for private development. The scientific content of Kubler's findings is not disclosed in the public documents.

A second investor — a wealthy private individual, name unrecorded — contributes **$12 million** to the project. This is the capital that makes construction possible. This person dies within a short time of the investment. Their money remains.

Kubler is retained as lead researcher. He and Ross enter into a working relationship that is, characteristically, one of unequal authority: Kubler has the expertise; Ross has the money, the connections, and the agenda.

## ERA II — Construction and Assembly
### The Ross Hotel Is Built
*1848–1852*

Construction takes four years, conducted in deliberate secrecy. The hotel is Gothic-Victorian in form, its architecture ornate enough that the true reason for its existence — a mining operation directly beneath it — is invisible from the outside and nearly invisible from within.

The cover works. The hotel is presented as a private retreat and retirement residence for select guests. Most of the guests, from the beginning, are on Ross's payroll.

**[Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)** is brought in as the hotel's chief engineer during this period. Among his contributions: a private elevator shaft running from the hotel's upper floors all the way down into what will become the **[Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md)**. The design is based on the Otis steam-driven elevator model of the era, heavily modified for the depth and purpose of the mine. It is the first thing in the hotel built for what the hotel is actually for, rather than what it appears to be.

---

### The Scientific Cohort Assembles
*1852 onward — staggered over years*

Scientists are recruited in sequence, not all at once: first you find the seed; then you study the seed; then you need specialists.

**[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)** is among the earliest arrivals. He has spent his retirement being retired against his will — abandoned by a field he helped build, his family gone, no garden left to tend. When [Ross](albert-ross.md) offers him the [Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md) — an entire two-story greenhouse on the hotel's second floor, purpose-built for cultivating and studying the [God Seed](god-seeds.md)-affected plant life — Eila does not deliberate long. He arrives and does not leave. He is the oldest living person in the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) before the Kubler even takes root in the walls.

**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)** takes up permanent residence as lead researcher, now operating out of the [Ingersole](ingersole-caverns.md) directly.

**[Dr. Elizabeth Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md)** is recruited as the cohort's entomologist and biological countermeasures specialist. She and **[Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md)** — the cohort's geneticist — develop a rivalry that becomes one of the hotel's defining long-running tensions. Sam is, by the accounting of anyone who has studied the [God Seeds](god-seeds.md), arguably the most responsible of the researchers for what the experiments became. He does not sleep easily with this. He does not sleep easily at all anymore. He has installed dream catchers across Room 509. His loop is to sleep and to dream and to not quite be able to stop.

Blueth establishes the [Insect Room](blueths-insect-room.md) and begins what will become decades of cataloguing. She and Sam are, despite everything, close.

**[Martha Gioseppy Waliger](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md)** joins the hotel's operations as its head of housekeeping and domestic organisation — the person who ran the hotel's interior function before [Barret](barret-aricson.md) consolidated that role. She is not a scientist. She is, however, the only person in the building with formal permission to harvest from [Eila](eila-bartlett.md)'s [Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md), because she is the only person [Baya](baya-ross.md) trusts to make the floral teas she loves. Martha knows the compositions. The [Tea Book](baya.md) is open on her workbench, always. One recipe in it does something to the [Kubler](the-kubler.md). She finds this out.

**[Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md)** is the hotel's lead manager — the operational authority who holds the key to the basement lock and knows where everything is. Her loop is the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md): she is there for her own birthday, drinks from opening to close, and cannot stop. Something outside the hotel still sends the deliveries in. The bar never runs dry. Nobody asks why.

**[Raeburn Chastel](raeburn-rae-chastel.md)** tends the bar. He is tall enough that his head grazes the ceiling. He is not a scientist. He has been here long enough that the distinction barely matters.

### Baya Falls Ill — The Cadwalladers Arrive
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated mid-to-late 1800s]*

[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s wife, **[Baya](baya-ross.md)**, falls gravely ill. Ross's response is immediate and characteristically resource-heavy: he recruits the best physician available.

**[Dr. Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)** refuses. He dislikes confined spaces and has no interest in relocating. Financial incentives change this. The condition of his relocation: his wife, fashion designer **[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md)**, must have a dedicated studio. Ross agrees. A tailoring room is built for her in the [Attic](attic.md).

They arrive together. Francena begins designing uniforms for the hotel's operation — the cover requires dressing. Daniel begins treating Baya. They are, for a period, two people in a functioning marriage inside a strange and isolated place.

Baya recovers. The accounts describe it as a miracle. It is not, entirely, a miracle.

## ERA III — The Experiments and Their Consequences
### The God Seeds Are Studied
*Decades post-1852*

The [God Seeds](god-seeds.md) — also catalogued by Kubler as *Tears of God* and *Immortality Seeds* — are living organisms found within the meteor. They are the source of the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) plant's existence: the fungal life form grew from the seeds' spores as a biological response to the environment, spreading through the rock and soil of Ross Valley and eventually into the hotel structure itself.

The Kubler's key property emerges during this research period: its spore field, when dense enough, prevents the biological death of organisms within it. The mechanism is symbiotic. The Kubler requires living hosts. It keeps them alive to maintain this.

The implications for immortality research are obvious. The ethical implications are also obvious. The cohort proceeds.

---

### Jone Ross Consumes a God Seed
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated late 1800s]*

[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s son, **[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md)**, is a child in the hotel when access to the seed samples is insufficiently restricted. He consumes one.

The transformation is not immediate and not clean. Jone does not become immortal in any conventional sense. The God Seed becomes part of his biology, and his biology becomes something that requires continuous external maintenance to survive — replacement of degrading tissue, transplanted material, ongoing intervention. He remains visually a child. He does not age outward. What happens to him inward is ongoing.

Ross, confronted with what his ambition has produced, accelerates the research rather than stops it. He is looking for a cure. He does not find one.

---

### Emerette Is Created
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated late 1800s to early 1900s]*

During an experiment in the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md), the research produces an outcome no one planned for. **[Emerette of the Deep](emerette-of-the-deep.md)** — a ten-meter creature, the first fully immortal organism to emerge from God Seed exposure — comes into being.

**[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)**, hotel manager and [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s closest friend, attempts to seal the mines to prevent Emerette from leaving the lower caverns. Emerette crushes Barret's head during the sealing. Barret dies. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) revives him. He is no longer who he was. The drapes he uses to cover his face from this point forward are not decorative.

**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)** is caught in the crossfire of the mine sealing. He does not emerge. Over years in the closed-off caverns — in darkness, in the Kubler's dense spore field, with only the miners who were also trapped — he transforms. The man who walks among the [City of Quitters](city-of-quitters.md) as its crow-person patriarch, **[Relbuk Derfla](relbuk-derfla.md)**, is what is left of Alfred Kubler. His name reversed. His shape changed. His belief that he could fly out of there — Icarus, without the altitude.

Ross, seeing the full result of his project, issues the order: no one leaves.

---

### The Lockdown
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated early 1900s]*

Barret's standing mission from this point: maintain the hotel, keep the inhabitants inside, receive Ross's correspondence, and keep [Jone](jone-ross.md) alive by whatever means available.

The [Lost Children](the-cursed-maids.md) begin arriving through a private foundation established by Ross — terminal cases, sent by desperate parents to a place that promises them life. The foundation is secret. The arrangement is that the children live. What it costs them to live is not in the agreement.

[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) is kept alive through this system. Adult tissue degrades quickly in him. The children's does not.

The hotel continues to operate, in its strange way. [Kane](aaron-kane.md), long since withdrawn from engineering into theater, begins his second career in the [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md). [Eila](eila-bartlett.md) tends the Sky Garden. [Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) plays solitaire. [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md) makes her dress. [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s neck grows.

## ERA IV — The Long Silence
### Kane and the Puppets
*[UNCONFIRMED — across decades, roughly 1900s–1970s]*

[Kane](aaron-kane.md), bored, begins building marionettes. Then more. Then — understanding the curse and the nature of what the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) has made of him — finds a way to remove parts of himself: tendons, muscle tissue, structural material. Each part becomes a puppet, each puppet controlled by him. By the time the game takes place, there are forty bodies. He is, in a fungal-creature sense, one distributed person. The [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md) has never once been empty.

---

### Blueth's Experiments
*[UNCONFIRMED — mid-1900s]*

[Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) makes two attempts to suppress the [Kubler](the-kubler.md):

**The Moonlight Aphids**: engineered to feed on Kubler growth. They ignore the Kubler entirely and wander the hotel for decades.

**The Firebirds**: engineered to hunt the aphids. They also fail. The line is made sterile. One bird — the last — is caged in a storage room and forgotten. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) keeps it alive. It waits.

---

### Jenna Martina's Birthday — Jone's First Death
*~1985*

The date that anchors the hotel's final moment of collective motion.

[Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md)'s birthday. The guests gather at the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md). There is something resembling a party. No one, after the fact, can fully articulate why everyone went.

That night, [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) has his first death — the God Seed inside him falters. His body begins to fail in ways that previous maintenance has not addressed. [Barret](barret-aricson.md) performs emergency surgery. The sources for the repair are the Lost Children in the hotel.

Around this same period, [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) writes his last letter to Barret. He said he was coming back. He said he was going to make things right.

**[Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md)** — one of the [Cursed Maids](the-cursed-maids.md) — uses a computer to document something. The record exists. Finding it is the ARG.

Ross does not come back.

## ERA V — The Present
### Forty Years of Nothing
*~1985 to present day*

The [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) has been, in every practical sense, frozen since the mid-1980s. No new arrivals through official channels. No departures. Barret tends his responsibilities. The Kubler continues. [Eila](eila-bartlett.md) is, by now, more tree than man.

---

### Amy Arrives
*Present day*

**[Amelia "Amy" Loevinger](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)** is found outside the hotel, dead, and put back together.

She wakes in the [Ventilation Maintenance Room](ventilation-maintenance-room-amys-room.md) with a set of keys she doesn't remember picking up. Her iPhone is in her jacket. No signal.

[Barret](barret-aricson.md) does not yet know the keys are missing.

## Notes for the Narrative Team
- Dates marked **[UNCONFIRMED]** are extrapolated from character ages, [Kole](kole-wadjet.md)'s dialogue, and story logic. These should be reviewed and pinned during production.
- The 12 million dollar founding investment and the anonymous investor's death are established but the investor's identity is not — this is intentional lore space.
- [Eila](eila-bartlett.md) being both Kubler's teacher and already-retired before the hotel opens creates a timeline compression. Eila's age should be treated as functionally indefinite — the oldest person in the building before the Kubler even existed there.
- Ross's departure and fate are deliberately unresolved in the main game. The ARG handles it.
