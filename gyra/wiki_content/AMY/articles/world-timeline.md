# World Timeline

The events of *Amy's Escape* span roughly 175 years — from a university research expedition in South America to a present-day hotel in which no one has moved toward an exit in forty years. The game itself takes place entirely in the final hours of that stillness.

[SECONDARY]
> **Reading note:** Characters under the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) immortality effect do not age normally. Visual age and actual age diverge over time. Where known, both are listed.

---

## ERA I — Before the Hotel
### The Crater and the Meteor
*~Early-to-mid 1800s*

In a remote crater in South America — later purchased outright and renamed **Ross Valley** — a university research team discovers an anomaly in the rock: organisms embedded in meteoric material that cannot be killed. Tissue samples exposed to lethal conditions regenerate. Nothing in the existing taxonomy explains it.

The lead researcher is **[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)**, a botanist and biologist. His professor and longtime mentor is **[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)**, already retired, already one of the foremost plant scientists of his era. Eila does not join the expedition. He is, by this point, old enough that most people assume he will not be doing much of anything anymore.

Kubler reports the find through academic channels. The paper does not travel far. But a summary of it reaches, through a chain of acquaintances, a young man from a wealthy oil family who has a degree, some ambition, and the financial capacity to act on both.

His name is **[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)**.

---

### Ross Acquires Ross Valley
*~1848*

Ross buys the entire lot. The price is significant. The acquisition is conducted quietly, recorded as a land purchase for private development. The scientific content of Kubler's findings is not disclosed in the public documents.

A second investor — a wealthy private individual, name unrecorded — contributes **$12 million** to the project. This is the capital that makes construction possible. This person dies within a short time of the investment. Their money remains.

Kubler is retained as lead researcher. He and Ross enter into a working relationship that is, characteristically, one of unequal authority: Kubler has the expertise; Ross has the money, the connections, and the agenda.

---

## ERA II — Construction and Assembly
### The Ross Hotel Is Built
*1848–1852*

Construction takes four years, conducted in deliberate secrecy. The hotel is Gothic-Victorian in form, its architecture ornate enough that the true reason for its existence — a mining operation directly beneath it — is invisible from the outside and nearly invisible from within.

The cover works. The hotel is presented as a private retreat and retirement residence for select guests. Most of the guests, from the beginning, are on Ross's payroll.

**[Aaron Kane](aaron-kane.md)** is brought in as the hotel's chief engineer during this period. Among his contributions: a private elevator shaft running from the hotel's upper floors all the way down into what will become the **[Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md)**. The design is based on the Otis steam-driven elevator model of the era, heavily modified for the depth and purpose of the mine. It is the first thing in the hotel built for what the hotel is actually for, rather than what it appears to be.

---

### The Scientific Cohort Assembles
*1852 onward — staggered over years*

Scientists are recruited in sequence, not all at once: first you find the seed; then you study the seed; then you need specialists.

**[Eila Bartlett](eila-bartlett.md)** is among the earliest arrivals. He has spent his retirement being retired against his will — abandoned by a field he helped build, his family gone, no garden left to tend. When [Ross](albert-ross.md) offers him the [Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md) — an entire two-story greenhouse on the hotel's second floor, purpose-built for cultivating and studying the [God Seed](god-seeds.md)-affected plant life — Eila does not deliberate long. He arrives and does not leave. He is the oldest living person in the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) before the Kubler even takes root in the walls.

**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)** takes up permanent residence as lead researcher, now operating out of the [Ingersole](ingersole-caverns.md) directly.

**[Dr. Elizabeth Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md)** is recruited as the cohort's entomologist and biological countermeasures specialist. She and **[Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md)** — the cohort's geneticist — develop a rivalry that becomes one of the hotel's defining long-running tensions. Sam is, by the accounting of anyone who has studied the [God Seeds](god-seeds.md), arguably the most responsible of the researchers for what the experiments became. He does not sleep easily with this. He does not sleep easily at all anymore. He has installed dream catchers across Room 509. His loop is to sleep and to dream and to not quite be able to stop.

Blueth establishes the [Insect Room](blueths-insect-room.md) and begins what will become decades of cataloguing. She and Sam are, despite everything, close.

**[Martha Gioseppy Waliger](martha-gioseppy-waliger.md)** joins the hotel's operations as its head of housekeeping and domestic organisation — the person who ran the hotel's interior function before [Barret](barret-aricson.md) consolidated that role. She is not a scientist. She is, however, the only person in the building with formal permission to harvest from [Eila](eila-bartlett.md)'s [Sky Garden](the-sky-garden.md), because she is the only person [Baya](baya-ross.md) trusts to make the floral teas she loves. Martha knows the compositions. The [Tea Book](baya.md) is open on her workbench, always. One recipe in it does something to the [Kubler](the-kubler.md). She finds this out.

**[Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md)** is the hotel's lead manager — the operational authority who holds the key to the basement lock and knows where everything is. Her loop is the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md): she is there for her own birthday, drinks from opening to close, and cannot stop. Something outside the hotel still sends the deliveries in. The bar never runs dry. Nobody asks why.

**[Raeburn Chastel](raeburn-rae-chastel.md)** tends the bar. He is tall enough that his head grazes the ceiling. He is not a scientist. He has been here long enough that the distinction barely matters.

---

### Baya Falls Ill — The Cadwalladers Arrive
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated mid-to-late 1800s]*

[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s wife, **[Baya](baya-ross.md)**, falls gravely ill. Ross's response is immediate and characteristically resource-heavy: he recruits the best physician available.

**[Dr. Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)** refuses. He dislikes confined spaces and has no interest in relocating. Financial incentives change this. The condition of his relocation: his wife, fashion designer **[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md)**, must have a dedicated studio. Ross agrees. A tailoring room is built for her in the [Attic](attic.md).

They arrive together. Francena begins designing uniforms for the hotel's operation — the cover requires dressing. Daniel begins treating Baya. They are, for a period, two people in a functioning marriage inside a strange and isolated place.

Baya recovers. The accounts describe it as a miracle. It is not, entirely, a miracle.

## ERA III — The Experiments and Their Consequences
### The God Seeds Are Studied
*Decades post-1852*

The [God Seeds](god-seeds.md) — also catalogued by Kubler as *Tears of God* and *Immortality Seeds* — are living organisms found within the meteor. They are the source of the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) plant's existence: the fungal life form grew from the seeds' spores as a biological response to the environment, spreading through the rock and soil of Ross Valley and eventually into the hotel structure itself.

The Kubler's key property emerges during this research period: its spore field, when dense enough, prevents the biological death of organisms within it. The mechanism is symbiotic. The Kubler requires living hosts. It keeps them alive to maintain this.

The implications for immortality research are obvious. The ethical implications are also obvious. The cohort proceeds.

---

### Jone Ross Consumes a God Seed
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated late 1800s]*

[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s son, **[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md)**, is a child in the hotel when access to the seed samples is insufficiently restricted. He consumes one.

The transformation is not immediate and not clean. Jone does not become immortal in any conventional sense. The God Seed becomes part of his biology, and his biology becomes something that requires continuous external maintenance to survive — replacement of degrading tissue, transplanted material, ongoing intervention. He remains visually a child. He does not age outward. What happens to him inward is ongoing.

Ross, confronted with what his ambition has produced, accelerates the research rather than stops it. He is looking for a cure. He does not find one.

---

### Emerette Is Created
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated late 1800s to early 1900s]*

During an experiment in the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md), the research produces an outcome no one planned for. **[Emerette of the Deep](emerette-of-the-deep.md)** — a ten-meter creature, the first fully immortal organism to emerge from God Seed exposure — comes into being.

**[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)**, hotel manager and [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md)'s closest friend, attempts to seal the mines to prevent Emerette from leaving the lower caverns. Emerette crushes Barret's head during the sealing. Barret dies. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) revives him. He is no longer who he was. The drapes he uses to cover his face from this point forward are not decorative.

**[Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md)** is caught in the crossfire of the mine sealing. He does not emerge. Over years in the closed-off caverns — in darkness, in the Kubler's dense spore field, with only the miners who were also trapped — he transforms. The man who walks among the [City of Quitters](city-of-quitters.md) as its crow-person patriarch, **[Relbuk Derfla](relbuk-derfla.md)**, is what is left of Alfred Kubler. His name reversed. His shape changed. His belief that he could fly out of there — Icarus, without the altitude.

Ross, seeing the full result of his project, issues the order: no one leaves.

---

### The Lockdown
*[UNCONFIRMED — estimated early 1900s]*

Barret's standing mission from this point: maintain the hotel, keep the inhabitants inside, receive Ross's correspondence, and keep [Jone](jone-ross.md) alive by whatever means available.

The [Lost Children](the-cursed-maids.md) begin arriving through a private foundation established by Ross — terminal cases, sent by desperate parents to a place that promises them life. The foundation is secret. The arrangement is that the children live. What it costs them to live is not in the agreement.

[Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) is kept alive through this system. Adult tissue degrades quickly in him. The children's does not.

The hotel continues to operate, in its strange way. [Kane](aaron-kane.md), long since withdrawn from engineering into theater, begins his second career in the [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md). [Eila](eila-bartlett.md) tends the Sky Garden. [Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) plays solitaire. [Francena](francena-cadwallader.md) makes her dress. [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s neck grows.

---

## ERA IV — The Long Silence
### Kane and the Puppets
*[UNCONFIRMED — across decades, roughly 1900s–1970s]*

[Kane](aaron-kane.md), bored, begins building marionettes. Then more. Then — understanding the curse and the nature of what the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) has made of him — finds a way to remove parts of himself: tendons, muscle tissue, structural material. Each part becomes a puppet, each puppet controlled by him. By the time the game takes place, there are forty bodies. He is, in a fungal-creature sense, one distributed person. The [Theater of Dreams](the-theater-of-dreams.md) has never once been empty.

---

### Blueth's Experiments
*[UNCONFIRMED — mid-1900s]*

[Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) makes two attempts to suppress the [Kubler](the-kubler.md):

**The Moonlight Aphids**: engineered to feed on Kubler growth. They ignore the Kubler entirely and wander the hotel for decades.

**The Firebirds**: engineered to hunt the aphids. They also fail. The line is made sterile. One bird — the last — is caged in a storage room and forgotten. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) keeps it alive. It waits.

---

### Jenna Martina's Birthday — Jone's First Death
*~1985*

The date that anchors the hotel's final moment of collective motion.

[Jenna Martina](jenna-martina.md)'s birthday. The guests gather at the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md). There is something resembling a party. No one, after the fact, can fully articulate why everyone went.

That night, [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) has his first death — the God Seed inside him falters. His body begins to fail in ways that previous maintenance has not addressed. [Barret](barret-aricson.md) performs emergency surgery. The sources for the repair are the Lost Children in the hotel.

Around this same period, [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) writes his last letter to Barret. He said he was coming back. He said he was going to make things right.

**[Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md)** — one of the [Cursed Maids](the-cursed-maids.md) — uses a computer to document something. The record exists. Finding it is the ARG.

Ross does not come back.

---

## ERA V — The Present
### Forty Years of Nothing
*~1985 to present day*

The [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) has been, in every practical sense, frozen since the mid-1980s. No new arrivals through official channels. No departures. Barret tends his responsibilities. The Kubler continues. [Eila](eila-bartlett.md) is, by now, more tree than man.

---

### Amy Arrives
*Present day*

**[Amelia "Amy" Loevinger](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)** is found outside the hotel, dead, and put back together.

She wakes in the [Ventilation Maintenance Room](ventilation-maintenance-room-amys-room.md) with a set of keys she doesn't remember picking up. Her iPhone is in her jacket. No signal.

[Barret](barret-aricson.md) does not yet know the keys are missing.

## Notes for the Narrative Team
- Dates marked **[UNCONFIRMED]** are extrapolated from character ages, [Kole](kole-wadjet.md)'s dialogue, and story logic. These should be reviewed and pinned during production.
- The 12 million dollar founding investment and the anonymous investor's death are established but the investor's identity is not — this is intentional lore space.
- [Eila](eila-bartlett.md) being both Kubler's teacher and already-retired before the hotel opens creates a timeline compression. Eila's age should be treated as functionally indefinite — the oldest person in the building before the Kubler even existed there.
- Ross's departure and fate are deliberately unresolved in the main game. The ARG handles it.
