*Amy's Escape* has five main endings, one hidden ending, and a special Genocide Run mode. The game never announces which ending is occurring. There is no score, no ending card. There is only what happens next.

Which ending fires is determined by three variables: the state of [Baya's tea](baya-ross.md), how many of the ten story puzzles were solved humanely, and a small number of specific actions. See [Endings](endings.md) for the full breakdown.

## The Endings

| Ending | Name | Short summary |
|---|---|---|
| [Ending F](ending-f.md) | **Perfect Night** | Save everyone. Give [Barret](barret-aricson.md) the cigar. "Thanks for playing." |
| [Endings A / B / C](endings-a.md) | **Half a Monster** | [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) throws [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) from the window. Three variations on the same loss. |
| [Ending E](ending-e.md) | **All Alone** | Fail everything. Leave. Nothing happens. Nothing at all. |
| [Ending D](ending-d.md) | **Fishbowl** | [Evangeline](evangeline-aerona-and-ilina.md) offers to take the pain away. You can say yes. |
| [Sad Escape Ending](sad-escape-ending.md) | **Sad Escape** | Unlocked by completing the ARG. |
| [Genocide Run](the-genocide-run-ending.md) | **Genocide Run** | A separate mode. You have to mean it. You get everything. Almost. |

## Achievements and the Genocide Run

Every bad-path solution across all ten story puzzles is an achievement. The [Genocide Run](the-genocide-run-ending.md) is the only way to collect all of them in a single playthrough; it is built for exactly this, and the game is aware of what you are doing.

You get every achievement. Except one.

The **Fishbowl** ([Ending D](ending-d.md)) achievement cannot be obtained on a genocide run. The run locks you out of it by design. The subversion: you did everything the game tracks as cruel and collectible, and the one thing it keeps from you is the ending that was about forgetting you ever were.

For the full achievement list and why the system works this way, see [Achievements](achievements.md).

---

## The Hidden Ending

The **Fishbowl** ([Ending D](ending-d.md)) is only reachable when Amy is already on the path to the worst outcome. It cannot be found by trying to find it. [Evangeline and Ilina](evangeline-aerona-and-ilina.md) know you are coming before you do.

---

## "Thanks for Playing"

Only [Ending F](ending-f.md) receives these words — after the credits, not before. The game earns them or it doesn't. Most playthroughs don't.
