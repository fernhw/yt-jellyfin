## Overview

Blueth spent years trying to design a biological countermeasure for the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) organism. The Moonlight Aphids were her 1945 attempt: insects engineered to feed on the Kubler's spore structures and suppress its spread. They were never released into the hotel environment. They were never released because, in testing, it became apparent they had no interest in the Kubler whatsoever. They ate other things. They glowed. They wandered.

All twelve are currently in sealed jars at various points throughout the hotel, left where they ended up or placed deliberately by someone, or both. They are small, fluffy, bioluminescent, and visually striking. They are the game's collectible.

## As a Collectible

The twelve jars are hidden throughout all five chapters, some behind puzzles and others in places that require careful exploration to reach. Collecting them is optional and does not affect the main ending. What it does to the [Kubler](the-kubler.md), what twelve released aphids that were engineered to suppress it but never tested at scale might do, is a question the game allows the player to answer by completing the set.

## Relation to the Firebirds

Blueth's second countermeasure attempt, in 1958, was the Firebird line, birds designed to hunt the aphids. They also failed. The last surviving Firebird is [Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md).
