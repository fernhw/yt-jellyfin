## Overview

Chapter 4 — *Sen* — corresponds to Depression in the [Kubler-Ross grief model](world-timeline.md). It is the shortest chapter. The name *Sen* — from the Japanese, meaning before, earlier, the past — indexes the chapter's mood: something drained, pulled downward, the grief that doesn't make noise.

## Structure

Amy escapes [Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md) in the [Ingersole Miners' Hospital](ingersole-caverns.md). Barret is slowed here — the spores in the hospital area react to him in ways they do not react to Amy. The escape sequence is the chapter's mechanical core. It is not a puzzle in the Chapter 2 sense. It is a run.

[Nettle Tenesea](nettle-tenesea.md) appears in the hospital if Amy flushed her in all eight locations throughout the hotel during Chapters 1–3. Her contribution to the escape is the kind of help that arrives in exactly the right moment and is not explained.

The chapter ends when Amy enters the [Ingersole Mines](ingersole-caverns.md) proper for the first time.

## Symbolic Role

Chapter 4 is the chapter where Amy stops moving forward and simply has to keep moving. There are no good and bad solutions. There is no sin accrual. There is just the hospital, the dark, and the door to the mines. The chapter's brevity is intentional. Depression as a grief stage is not dramatic. It is the absence of drama. The game respects that.
