## Overview

The Cursed Maids and the [Lost Children](definitions.md) are closely related groups. The Lost Children were sent to the hotel by families who were promised their terminal cases would be cured; what happened to them instead became the mechanism that keeps [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) alive. The Cursed Maids are the hotel's operational staff — people who came to work and were never able to leave, their minds and bodies gradually reshaped by the Kubler field and the loops.

They are aware. That is the part that distinguishes them from furniture. They know what the hotel has done to them and what they are now. Most of them have accommodated it, in the same way that the miners accommodated the mines — not happily, but completely.

## Melina

[Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md) was a Cursed Maid and the first to seriously attempt escape. She documented what she knew in 1985, using a computer — a record that survived her. She addressed every note with "Well Done Moniker," giving whoever found them a small, deliberate acknowledgment. She eventually lost her legs attempting to run and, with them, her will to continue. Her notes became an ARG.

## Notable Members

The numbered maids in the hotel's records do not map cleanly to people — the hotel has cycled through staff in ways that blur the accounting. Melina is the most documented. Others are known by their numbers or partial names, their full identities consumed by the loops they were caught in.
