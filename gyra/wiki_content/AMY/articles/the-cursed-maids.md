[MAIN_SECTION]
The Cursed Maids are the children who were brought to the hotel under false promises of treatment, cure, or salvation, and who never left. Over time, the [Kubler](the-kubler.md), the loops, and the hotel's rules reshaped them into domestic servants.

They are more than a baker's dozen in number and fewer than a hundred. The exact count is not fixed; it depends on how many the story needs.

[SECONDARY]
## Overview

The Cursed Maids were sent to the hotel by families who were promised their terminal cases would be cured. What happened to them instead became part of the mechanism that keeps [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md) alive.

The hotel did not hire them in any normal sense. It kept them, looped them, hollowed them out, and eventually assigned them roles. "Maid" became less of a job and more of a condition imposed by the hotel.

They are aware. That is the part that distinguishes them from furniture. They know what the hotel has done to them and what they are now. Most of them have accommodated it, in the same way that the miners accommodated the mines, not happily, but completely.

## Melina

[Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md) was a Cursed Maid and the first to seriously attempt escape. She documented what she knew in 1985 using a computer, leaving behind a record that survived her. She addressed every note with "Well Done Moniker," giving whoever found them a small, deliberate acknowledgment.

She eventually lost her legs attempting to run and, with them, her will to continue. Her notes became an ARG.

## Notable Members

The numbered maids in the hotel's records do not map cleanly to people. The hotel has cycled through names, bodies, roles, and loops in ways that blur the accounting. Melina is the most documented. Others are known by their numbers or partial names, their full identities consumed by the loops they were caught in.
