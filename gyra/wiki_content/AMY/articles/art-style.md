# Art Style

The game's unique, cartoonish real-time 3D art style, inspired by films such as Lupin 3D and Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse, takes cues from Ghibli's use of Ligne Claire, complex dense backgrounds, and simple yet charming characters to make the game readable. We also consistently include worldbuilding elements in every drawer and painting.
