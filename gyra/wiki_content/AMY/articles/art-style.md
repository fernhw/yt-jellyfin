[MAIN_SECTION]
Real-time 3D. Inspired by Lupin III and Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse. Complex, densely detailed backgrounds. Simple, solid-color characters.

The contrast is the point. Complex backgrounds give the world weight and history. Simple characters make them readable against that complexity at all times. This is visual hierarchy as a design tool — not an aesthetic choice, a functional one. In a stealth game where you need to read character position instantly, it is necessary.

Worldbuilding lives in the backgrounds: every drawer, every painting, every room has detail that rewards looking.

[SECONDARY]
## Notes on the Approach

Ligne Claire as a term does not apply here. The concept that applies is visual hierarchy through contrast: dense, intricate world vs. clean, readable characters. The influence is the principle, not the line style.

Solid colors on characters are not a simplification. They are the mechanism by which the characters stay legible against the most detailed background in any given scene. This matters most in the stealth sections, where reading Amy's position relative to an enemy is a split-second read.

The 3D execution means the backgrounds can carry real architectural depth, shadow complexity, and environmental storytelling without flattening. The characters sit in that space without competing with it.
