# Symbology of endings

She confronts her inner demons, especially the haunting memory of her brother. Although players see Amy and Ross interacting, it's symbolic of her past mistakes. Baya plays a crucial role, especially towards the end when Amy confronts someone dear to her. The moment Amy enters Ross' sanctum, she's facing her deepest fears, which also means being blocked from her emotional core. The ending where Amy confronts Ross signifies her letting go of her brother's memory, causing her cherished yet burdensome memories to fade. While endings are designed to evoke emotions, they might not always provide closure. The truly fulfilling conclusions are hidden as secret endings. Ready to dive in?
