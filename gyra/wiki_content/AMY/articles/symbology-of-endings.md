[MAIN_SECTION]
[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) confronts her inner demons, especially the haunting memory of her brother [Custard](custard-loevinger.md). Although players see [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) and [Ross](albert-ross.md) interacting, it's symbolic of her past mistakes. [Baya](baya-ross.md) plays a crucial role, especially towards the end when Amy confronts someone dear to her. The moment Amy enters [Ross](albert-ross.md)' sanctum, she's facing her deepest fears, which also means being blocked from her emotional core. The ending where [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) confronts [Ross](albert-ross.md) signifies her letting go of her brother's memory, causing her cherished yet burdensome memories to fade. While endings are designed to evoke emotions, they might not always provide closure. The truly fulfilling conclusions are hidden as secret endings.
