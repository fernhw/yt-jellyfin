The confrontation with [Ross](albert-ross.md) is symbolic of Amy's relationship with her own past. [Baya](baya-ross.md)'s role becomes most significant at the chapter's end. When Amy enters Ross's room, she's not just facing him — she's facing the part of herself she's been avoiding. The endings aren't designed to give closure. The truly fulfilling ones are hidden.
