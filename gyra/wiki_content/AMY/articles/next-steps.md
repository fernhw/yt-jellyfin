# Next Steps

To successfully develop and release Amy's Escape, we will:

Secure funding for the total budget of $812,000-$12,000,000.
Expand our team to 10-20 members, including game developers, artists, and sound designers.
Develop a detailed production timeline and allocate resources accordingly.
Launch marketing campaigns, engage with influencers, and establish gaming media partnerships.
Attend industry events such as PAX, GDC, and Gamescom to showcase the game and network with industry professionals.
Obtain dev kits for PS5, Switch, and Xbox Series X and ensure proper release on these platforms.
Complete the development of Amy's Escape, ensuring high-quality gameplay, visuals, and sound design.
Release the game on targeted platforms and distribute it through appropriate channels.
Continue to engage with the gaming community, providing updates, bug fixes, and addressing player feedback.
Develop and release DLCs and expansions to further enrich the game's experience, keep Top Of Mind, toying with the community.
