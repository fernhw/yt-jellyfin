[MAIN_SECTION]
"Awoken"
Amy breaks out of her room at the Ross Hotel and searches for a way out. After a failed escape attempt, Kole Wadjet greets her and informs her that the keys to the hotel gates are on the top floor, where Mr. Ross lives. Amy has to go up. She meets the mute Daniel Cadwallader, who, after an encounter with his wife, tells Amy in writing that the only way to get through is through the elevator. The generator that powers it is at the bottom of the hotel, deep in the Ingersole.

[SECONDARY]
## Chapter Name
**Raphe.** The word carries two meanings: the vascular ridge found in a seed, the seam where it first opens, and the biological term for a join of tissues that weren't originally connected. It is a chapter about a girl whose pieces are still settling into place.

The GDD recorded the working title *Awoken*. The final title is **Raphe**.

---

## Stages of Grief Parallel
Each chapter in *Amy's Escape* maps to one of the **five stages of grief**, expressed through the imagery of a plant part. Chapter 1 (Raphe) is **Denial**. The seed, sealed. The seam, forced. [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) will not examine what she is doing here or why. She will find a way out. That is all.

---

## Tone
The chapter opens in a rare state of optimism. The tutorial is built inside it deliberately — the player learns the systems alongside Amy's momentum, in a brief window where escape seems genuinely possible. Then the camera cuts. The hope collapses without explanation. The game never explains what happened at that door.

Darby returns. Amy stands up. The rest of the chapter is quieter than the opening promised.

## Chapter Beats

### The Exit
[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) is in the [Ventilation Maintenance Room](ventilation-maintenance-room-amys-room.md), her room inside the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md)'s duct system, striped with vent-cast shadows. She has been serving at the hotel for an unspecified number of days. Tonight she stole [Barret](barret-aricson.md)'s keys. They are already in her inventory. The game teaches items, doors, and [Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md)'s mechanics here in a natural, momentum-driven sequence. Amy reaches the exit. The keys fit. She opens it.

The camera cuts. Close on her hands, reassembly scars healing in real time, evidence of what the hotel put back together before she woke. She is outside. Something is wrong with the outside. She sits down.

[Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md), unable to cross the threshold (biologically extinct, kept alive only by the [Kubler](the-kubler.md)'s immortality field, cannot leave), returns and lands on her. Amy gets up. *"Even if I lose everything."* She goes back inside.

### The Hotel — Early Exploration
[Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md) has not yet discovered the missing keys. [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) has the run of the hotel in relative safety. No one truly sleeps here; guests persist in their private eternities. Significant optional exploration is available, including early access to parts of [Chapter 2](chapter-2.md)'s floor plan, though those areas cannot be fully resolved without the elevator active.

### Kole Wadjet — The Lobby
[Kole Wadjet](kole-wadjet.md) staffs the [Lobby](the-lobby.md) reception desk with the calm of someone who has concluded nothing will surprise him. He confirms the facts plainly: the exit keys are with [Mr. Ross](albert-ross.md), top floor, elevator needed, elevator dead, generator below in the [Ingersole](ingersole-caverns.md). He does not editorialize. He will answer other questions about the hotel and its residents in the same tone: accurate, minimal, uninvested.

### Room 509 — Sam Bernal
[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)'s cleaning duster is in Room 509. [Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md) is asleep in it, not just napping, but genuinely, deeply unconscious for what [Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) estimates has been several days. When Amy climbs the shelf to retrieve the duster, [Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md) gets trapped beneath him. Without Darby, the [Kubler](the-kubler.md)-infected [Attic](attic.md) passage burns. Sam must be woken.

**The Sam Puzzle** has three solutions:

| Solution | Method | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **The Lamp** | Set his oil lamp alight; smoke and heat wake him | He wakes badly rattled; part of the hotel scorches. **[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)'s first [Sin Mark](sin-system.md): soot painted on her.** [Sam](sam-bernal.md) does not appear in the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md). |
| **His Fear** | Learn his greatest fear from [Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md); confront him with it | He wakes startled but recovers. Moderate outcome. |
| **The Long Talk** | Sit with [Sam](sam-bernal.md) and speak with him for 4–5 minutes | He wakes naturally, feeling heard. **Best outcome.** Sam joins the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md) if other conditions are met. |

### Dr. Blueth Needham — The Spider Door
One door on Floor 5 is covered in cobwebs, cleared by the duster. Inside: [Dr. Elizabeth Blueth Needham](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md), playing solitaire at a table, her cards so faded with age they're nearly colorless. She is polite but closed.

**Optional — The Solitaire Cards:** Two missing cards from her deck are scattered in the hotel's common areas. Amy can find and return them. Blueth looks at her newly-complete deck, realizes it was *always* unwinnable, and kicks it across the floor in quiet fury. This breaks her isolation. She opens up. She tells Amy what [Sam](sam-bernal.md) fears most.

### The Attic — Cadwallader
The [Attic](attic.md) has a [Kubler](the-kubler.md) presence, red-flowering patches along the walls that burn on contact and smell of rotting meat. [Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md)'s bioluminescence suppresses the active stage temporarily, making navigation possible.

[Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) finds a box she needs moved. The thing helping her move it is [Dr. Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md): a head, a long neck descending from the ceiling, no eyes, no mouth. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) grew his neck from his guilt. His body is on the other side of a wall.

Through nods, shakes, pauses, and patient back-and-forth, they communicate. He leads her to a poster. Behind it: a door, light at the edges. He does not say it is safe. His body is behind it.

Reunited, he writes one instruction: *the elevator*. Go down. He does not explain what is there. He sends Amy toward it because of what he did there. His regret is her mission.

### Francena's Dress (Optional Puzzle)
[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md) works in the adjacent tailoring room, eighty years into constructing a dress for a party she never attended. Players can ignore her, trap her in a corner (negative consequence for [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s arc), or engage fully, learning the story of the party and helping her reach the design she's been chasing. Full engagement puts her in the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md).

## Design Notes
- [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) mentions [Custard](custard-loevinger.md) exactly once in this chapter, off-handedly, as though it's a small, silly story. She does not linger on it.
- [Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md) is present in the hotel but unaware the keys are missing. His discovery is the inciting pressure of [Chapter 2](chapter-2.md).
- The smell of the [Kubler](the-kubler.md)'s red flowers is described as rotting meat. The reason is not revealed in Chapter 1.
- The [Sin System](sin-system.md) begins here: each bad-path solution leaves a physical mark on [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) (soot from the lamp fire, for example) that persists visually. These marks are also the game's [achievement](achievements.md) triggers for bad decisions.
