## Overview

Martha was [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md)'s personal housekeeper for as long as anyone in the hotel can reliably remember. Her particular expertise was tea, specifically the sourcing, preparation, and personalisation of tea blends for Baya's very specific preferences. She maintained a Tea Book, hand-annotated over decades, cataloguing every blend she had prepared, every modification, every note Baya had given her.

## What Happened to Martha

Martha's transformation into a shrub was accidental, instantaneous, and complete. She was developing a new recipe, one of her own design, experimenting beyond the standard catalogue, when the preparation reacted unexpectedly with the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) organism's ambient spores. The entire process took minutes. She was a shrub by the time anyone thought to check on her.

She is still in her room. The Tea Book is still open beside her, on the exact page of the recipe that caused it.

## In Gameplay

Martha's room and her Tea Book are central to the [Chapter 2](chapter-2.md) Baya puzzle. There are three solutions to dealing with [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md)'s appetite, and two of them require the Tea Book: Baya's favourite blend (the good solution, hidden in the Book's annotated entries) and the stone tea (the bad solution, which turns Baya into a very large plant and is itself the recipe that destroyed Martha, now discovered by Amy and applied to someone else). The third solution, sleepy tea, is easy to find and not in Martha's book.

## Symbolic Role

Martha is a small tragedy in the background of a chapter about larger ones: a person who was extremely good at their specific thing, undone by the same thing they were good at. The Tea Book being open next to her, still readable, is a quiet visual detail.
