# Reason: Flexibility, Expandability, Cross-Platform Compatibility, Artistic Style

Unity is a versatile game engine that enables developers to create exceptional games, such as INSIDE and Genshin Impact. Choosing Unity offers various advantages, including performance optimization through its Data-Oriented Technology Stack (DOTS), a vast community providing pre-made resources, and cross-platform compatibility. It also excels at supporting artistic and stylized visuals, allowing developers to create unique and engaging experiences. Games of Note: INSIDE, GENSHIN IMPACT, Hollow Knight, Pokemon Go!, Cuphead, Ori and the Blind Forest, Oxenfree.
