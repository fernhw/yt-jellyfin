## Overview

The Liminal Bar occupies a space that is technically part of the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) and technically not. It is a bar. It looks like one. The supplies arrive on their own schedule from somewhere that has never been identified. Nobody who drinks there is concerned by this. They are already in a hotel where nobody dies and nothing ends.

Characters who receive good solutions to their story puzzles are sent to the Liminal Bar. They arrive there, intact, and they can interact with each other. The bar accumulates residents as [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) makes better choices — it is the game's visible ledger of what was saved rather than what was lost.

## Statuettes

Certain residents of the hotel are too large to enter the bar in their physical form. [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md) at twelve metres, [Julianne Wellington](julianne-wellington.md) at nine metres — the door does not accommodate them. For characters in this situation, a small statuette of them appears in the bar instead, placed on the counter or a shelf. The statuette is accurate in detail. It represents them. They know it's there. See also: [Statuettes for Larger Characters](statuettes-for-larger-characters.md).

## The Supply Question

The bar's deliveries continue. This is a detail the game surfaces and does not resolve. Something external to the hotel — outside its Kubler field, outside its loop — is aware enough of the bar's existence to keep it running. What that means about the hotel's relationship to the outside world is left open.
