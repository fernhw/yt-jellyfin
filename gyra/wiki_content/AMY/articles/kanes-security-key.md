[MAIN_SECTION]
Another set of collectibles in the game are the three pieces of Kane's Key. To obtain them, players must solve intricate puzzles left by Kane himself. Once the key is assembled and Kane's room is accessed, Amy can acquire the Puppet's Mask and Kane's hidden virtuous side, a puppet concealed within the room. These items enable players to bypass the Theater of Dreams minigame and reach the City of Quitters, accompanied by Mini Kane who communicates through the mask.
