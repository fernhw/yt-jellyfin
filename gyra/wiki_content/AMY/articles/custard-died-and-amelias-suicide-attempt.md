# Custard Died And Amelia's Suicide Attempt

Not shown in the story, this is for players who really read into it and find the clues and secrets. Amy wants to kill herself. The story lies TO the player. What really happened was a freak accident with an 8-year-old. Custard Loevinger, Amelia's brother, climbed too high and fell from a tree. Amy, wrought with guilt, couldn't face her family. She killed herself. Barry finds her, and the story of the game starts. Amy represses her brother's death. The real goal of the story is to make her realize that life is worth something. Amy wants to leave the hotel to die in peace. Amy wants to die. Every lie is meant to put Amy in a purgatory of her own creation; she has to learn to forgive herself. This is not shown in the story. Instead, it is obvious in chapters 2 and 3 that Amy is hiding something from everyone and isn't afraid of dying to an extreme extent. It is revealed through a sound flashback hidden in the game that she was crying and couldn't wake her brother up.
