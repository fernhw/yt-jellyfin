# Tragedies

Instead of just dying and restarting, players experience the harrowing outcomes of their choices, which can result in gruesome or psychologically disturbing situations for Amy. These tragedies heighten the stakes and make the gameplay experience even more immersive and emotionally engaging. Death doesn't exist in this world, only horrors.
