## Overview
Amy's room is not a room in any guest-register sense. It is a maintenance chamber built into the hotel's ventilation infrastructure, a functional space accessed through ductwork, where the mechanical respiration of the building is a constant presence. The walls are crossed with vent-cast shadows that shift as pressure changes move through the system.

She keeps it tidy.

---

## In the Story
The game opens here. [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) wakes in this room, fully dressed, with an odd set of keys already in her inventory. No explanation is given for the keys or for how she came to be in the room. The space establishes the game's visual register: detailed, beautiful in its strangeness, quiet in a way that precedes something.

The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) has not reached this room. The ventilation keeps it clean. This is not accidental.

---

## Layout & Access
The room connects to the hotel's primary duct system, which Amy can use for limited traversal throughout the game. The vent access point requires the cleaning duster to navigate in some configurations (dust buildup blocks passage). The room has one door to the main hall and one vent panel that serves as an alternate entrance.

Room 509, [Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md)'s room, is a short distance down the same floor.

---

## Notes
- The shifting vent-shadow patterns in the room's opening moments communicate Amy's fractured state without exposition. The animation team used the shadow movement to externalize what isn't being said.
- Whoever placed Amy in this room made a specific choice about it. The ventilation keeps the Kubler out. That is not standard hotel accommodation.
