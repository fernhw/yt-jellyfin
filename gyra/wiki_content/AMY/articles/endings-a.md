# Endings A, B, And C — Half A Monster

*Three variations on the same failure. The most common ending family. Designed to send you back in.*

**Base conditions:** Baya's tea in a bad state, and failing 4 or more of the 10 story puzzles.

---

## Ending A — Half a Monster (most common)

Amy confronts [Ross](albert-ross.md). There is an argument. Amy pushes him from the window. Ross's body hits the floor below and cracks open like a vase.

This breaks the hotel's curse. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) loses its anchor. The hotel deflates — not dramatically, just quietly. People can move again. They don't know what to do with that.

Amy does not feel like she won. This scene mirrors what she was told about her brother. She didn't change who she is. She just proved it.

[Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md) is not here. If the player tries to summon Darby, the game responds: *Darby isn't here anymore.*

Amy walks out. The game does not comment.

---

## Ending B — The Regret Ending

**Condition variation:** Don't poison Baya's tea. Fail enough puzzles. Enter Ross's room.

There is no confrontation. Ross killed himself before Amy arrived. The body is there. It is broken like a vase. He did not wait to be pushed.

The world has been without music for the entire approach to this ending — faint, easy to miss, the kind of silence that hints rather than announces. Most players will not notice until they read about it.

Amy does not get to be angry. There is no one to be angry at. She leaves.

---

## Ending C — The WTF Ending

**Condition variation:** Amy eats the [Kubler seed](god-seeds.md) before reaching Ross.

She feels a stomach ache as she approaches him. Ross feels something too — he doesn't know what it is, but he feels it.

When Amy touches Ross, they fuse. Amy is stronger. She absorbs him. They become a single, eternal thing.

The cutscene is two seconds of body horror. Then Amy, white-haired now, standing in the [penthouse](ross-penthouse.md). The Kubler curse is stable. Amy is the new owner of the Ross Hotel. Ross is dissolved inside her.

Credits.

[SECONDARY]
## Notes

- Ending A is the most common ending on first playthrough. It is designed to be. The puzzle fail threshold (4/10) is easy to cross without meaning to.
- The mirroring of Custard's death in Ending A is intentional and unspoken. The game will not point it out.
- Ending C is the game's only body horror moment rendered on screen. Everything else is implied.
- All three A/B/C variations have the same achievement category — completing any of them is an achievement, not a reward.
