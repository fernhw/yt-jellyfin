[MAIN_SECTION]
[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) spent years looking for seeds he believed were left in the ground millions of years ago by something that wasn't human. He dug the Ingersole to find them and brought in a group of scientists to study what he found.

Two of the worst things in the hotel came from that work. The experiments created [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md). [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md), Albert's son, ate one of the seeds by accident and became something that needs other people's organs to keep running.

Amy can find two seeds hidden in the game. Eating one makes her the new matriarch of the hotel in a special version of [Ending A](endings-a.md).

Not eating them and taking the good ending adds a final beat: *"In the end, I found Custard; my parents got mad at us but we returned home."* Her brother is brought back. The audience doesn't know how. The player has to put it together before the dread hits.
