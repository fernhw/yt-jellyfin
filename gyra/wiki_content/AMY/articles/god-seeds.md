[MAIN_SECTION]
[Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) desperately sought seeds that were left in the zone millions of years ago by supposed gods or creatures from the beyond. He created a mine to find them and called a group of scientists to study their properties. In their experiments, they accidentally created [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md), a 10-meter-tall monster, and [Jone Ross](jone-ross.md), Albert's son, consumed one of the seeds by mistake, turning him into a thing that needed body parts transplanted from other people to survive. Despite the problems encountered during the experiments, [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) may still come across two of these seeds hidden throughout the map in the game. The seeds will change the ending; eating one will make you the matriarch of the hotel in a special version of [Ending A](endings-a.md).

Not eating them will cause a happy ending to have a twist: "In the end, I found Custard; my parents got mad at us but we returned home" Her brother is brought back to life with the seeds, without the audience knowing this. The implications are disgusting. But its great to see that the player has to put 2 and 2 together before feeling any dread.
