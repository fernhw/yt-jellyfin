## Overview

Julianne Wellington is a man: a baker, nine metres tall, bearded, and deeply uncomfortable with being nine metres tall. He is not in the hotel because he wants to be. He is in the hotel because he grew too large to leave, and by the time that happened, [Clementine](clementine-wellington.md) was still there, and leaving Clementine was apparently the less acceptable option.

He is scared. He is timid. He does not carry his size with the authority that [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md) carries hers or the threat that [Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md) carries his. He is a very large man who became very large by accident and has not quite reconciled himself to it.

## The Quarrel

Julianne and [Clementine](clementine-wellington.md) have been in a long, sustained lovers' quarrel for most of their time at the hotel. He hears what he wants to hear. She hears what she wants to hear. The specifics of the argument change; the structure of it never does. Being nine metres tall has not made him better at standing his ground in it. Possibly the opposite.

## In Gameplay and Symbolism

Julianne's area is a door and a hallway from [Kane's theater](the-theater-of-dreams.md), placing him at the junction of [Chapter 2](chapter-2.md) and [Chapter 3](chapter-3.md). He maps symbolically to both Anger and Bargaining: he is angry about his situation in the way that people who are also terrified tend to be angry, and he is perpetually in a state of negotiation with his circumstances. The quarrel with Clementine enacts both. He bargains. She gets angrier. He gets more scared. Round and round.
