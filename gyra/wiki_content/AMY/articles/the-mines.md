# The Mines

a Rapture of the Forest
The entire hotel is a façade for the true purpose of the area, which is a mine where immortal seeds, the God's Seeds, are sought. The hotel was constructed with certain specifications to prevent guests from discovering the mines and alerting the authorities. But 90% of the guests were on Ross' payroll. Back in the 1850s people weren't so keen on operations like these so Ross wisely kept it a secret, his own Rapture.
