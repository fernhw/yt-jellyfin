[MAIN_SECTION]
This mechanic epitomizes the blending of the game's perspectives. While players predominantly observe Amy's journey in third person, they're frequently given glimpses into her direct experiences. When she holds an item, not only is she seen holding it, but an overlay of a hand - a phantom representation of Amy's sensation - emerges on the screen, grasping the item. It creates an intimate connection, allowing players to not only see but also feel Amy's actions, shattering the 4th wall.
