# Replayability

You're not meant to see everything; in fact, you can't. With the approach to puzzles, your fate is sealed once you solve something. Amy can reach her final goal, but you will want to go back and replay the game to find what happens if you didn't do a bad thing. Inspired by Majora's Mask's storytelling, we make the meat of the story in how action windows expire, making players consider the variables and routes for the perfect run. We also put secrets as we reward players for trying AND achieving this perfect run.
Amy's Escape also offers multiple difficulty levels with entirely different puzzle solutions and AI exploration routes, encouraging players to replay the game and discover new challenges.
