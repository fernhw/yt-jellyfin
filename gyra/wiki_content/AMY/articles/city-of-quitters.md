# City Of Quitters

Also called **Ingersole's Embrace**. A settlement built inside the deepest sealed section of the [Ingersole Caverns](ingersole-caverns.md) by the miners who refused to leave after the 1892 sealing.

## What It Is

After [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) fully manifested below the mines, the surface decision was to seal the Ingersole operation and walk away. Not all the miners agreed to leave. Some stayed, either by choice, by loyalty to the work, or because they could not find the exit in time. The [Kubler](the-kubler.md) organism's reach extends this deep, so they did not die. They changed.

Over generations, extended exposure to the Kubler spores and proximity to [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) deformed the miners into smaller, denser creatures. They are not quite human anymore. Their numbers dwindle. The ones who survive have built an improvised city lit entirely by artificial light. [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) cannot enter illuminated space.

The city is unstable. Sections collapse. Light sources fail and are replaced. It is a city held together by stubbornness and the knowledge that the moment the lights go out permanently, it ends.

## Governance

[Relbuk Derfla](relbuk-derfla.md) leads the City of Quitters. He is a crow-human hybrid, the product of long Kubler mutation, and the longest-surviving resident. He is not kind, but he is competent. He maintains the light grid and the social order with equal attention to both.

## Amy's Involvement

The City of Quitters is the central location of [Chapter 5](chapter-5.md). [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) arrives through the caverns and must earn the trust of the remaining residents to mount any kind of action against [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md).

If Amy abandons the city and runs to the VIP elevator, [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) is not stopped. The deformed miners are not killed outright. They continue to dwindle, their numbers dropping further as Emerette moves through the remaining lit sections. The city survives in a worse state. This is the *Skip Town* bad path and triggers the achievement.

If Amy uses the mirror network to direct sunlight into the mines and burns [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md), the city is freed from the immediate threat. The residents who can walk appear at the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md). [Relbuk](relbuk-derfla.md) joins the bar crowd. The [God Seed](god-seeds.md) becomes accessible.

## The Skip

A secret route allows [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) to bypass the city entirely via the VIP elevator shaft. Using it does not protect the city. [Emerette](emerette-of-the-deep.md) continues. The city's fate is the same as the abandonment path.

## Notes

- The city's name is self-deprecating. "We quit the surface" is how the original miners described their choice, not as defeat but as rejection.
- [Dr. Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md) operated the mine hospital near the upper entrance to the city. The hospital is where [Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md)'s encounter takes place before the city proper.
- The artificial light system is [Relbuk](relbuk-derfla.md)'s primary obsession and the thing he has spent the most years maintaining.
- The [Ingersole mystery](wip-the-ingersole-mystery.md) surrounding the original sealing is documented separately.
