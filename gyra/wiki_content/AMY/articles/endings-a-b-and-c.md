# Endings A, B, and C:

Half a Monster Ending variations (A: most common)
Conditions: poison Baya's tea, AND fail 4/10 of the story puzzles.
Summary: Amy throws Ross out of a window, ends the curse, but feels awful.
Amy confronts Mr. Ross and pushes him to the window below. Ross's body cracks open like a vase falling to his doom; this breaks the curse of the hotel, but at the same time, Amy feels regret. This scene mirror's her brother's alleged demise in the back story; she doesn't change who she is. The bird, inexplicably, isn't there. Amy walks. Notes: If the player summons Darby, it will say Darby isn't here anymore. This is a bad ending. Its designed so people do it all over again.
Variations: changes cutscene.
B) Don't poison Baya; you enter, and a person is broken like a vase. Ross killed himself. Does not confront you. the world being without music as you go inside hints at this. This is the regret-ending
C) Amy eats the Kubler seed; she feels a tummy ache as she approaches Ross. Ross too feels pain, but when Amy touches Ross, they fuse. Amy, being stronger, absorbs Ross, and they become eternal. Cutscene is just 2 seconds of body horror, and Amy is white-haired now. Kubler Curse stable now; Amy is the owner of the Ross Hotel. Ross gets dissolved in this. This is the wtf ending
