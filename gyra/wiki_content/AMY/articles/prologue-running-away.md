[MAIN_SECTION]
Amy wakes up from her room and sees where to go next

Amy wakes in the Ventilation Maintenance Room — her room inside the Ross Hotel's duct system, striped with vent-cast shadows. An odd set of keys is already in her inventory. The game teaches items, doors, and Darby's mechanics here in a natural, momentum-driven sequence. Amy reaches the exit. The keys fit. She opens it.

The camera cuts. Close on her hands — reassembly scars, healing in real time, evidence of what the hotel put back together before she woke. She is outside. Something is wrong with the outside. She sits down.

Darby — unable to cross the threshold (biologically extinct, kept alive only by the Kubler's immortality field, cannot leave) — returns and lands on her. Amy gets up. "Even if I lose everything." She goes back inside.
