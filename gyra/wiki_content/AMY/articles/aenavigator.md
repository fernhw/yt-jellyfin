## Why It Was Built

The game's navigable spaces are divided into arenas — distinct scene units with their own geometry, layers, and nav mesh. Switching between them in Unity's default editor was slow. AENavigator was built to make that fast.

It sits in the editor and gives the team a direct list of all arena scenes. Click one, you're in it. Layer visibility is controlled from the same panel — no hunting through the inspector. This is the difference between iteration taking seconds and iteration taking minutes, multiplied across a full dev cycle.

Anything that is navigable needs to be in a layer for the controls to work. That is the only rule.
