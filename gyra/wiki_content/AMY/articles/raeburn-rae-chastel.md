## Overview

Raeburn Chastel is the bartender at the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md). He is four metres tall. He calls himself Rae, which is a reduction of Raeburn that he has probably stopped consciously choosing and is simply now what he is called.

His relationship with [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) is uncomplicated: she is fourteen years old, she would like a drink, and he will not give her one. He is pleasant about it. The refusal is complete. This will not change regardless of how Amy approaches the situation, which is, in a hotel full of puzzles with multiple solutions, something of a statement.

Raeburn is not a puzzle. He is a person doing his job in circumstances that stopped making sense a very long time ago, who has retained the part about not serving alcohol to children as a fixed point. In a game full of things that have bent and warped and transformed beyond recognition, Rae's position on this is oddly comforting.
