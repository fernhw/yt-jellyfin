# The God seeds

also named Tear of God and Immortality Seed, These metaphysical miracles found in the ingersole mines—they're the fantastical element that drives the story; harnessing or curing these breathing organisms creates the kubler, or even considered the source of the kubler. But as any organism it needs to eat is found to be very adaptable, the kubler will figure out how to survive by all means necessary.
