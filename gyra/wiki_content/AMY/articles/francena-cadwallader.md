[SECONDARY]
## Overview
[Francena Cadwallader](francena-cadwallader.md) occupies the [Attic](attic.md) tailoring room with the complete focus of someone who has not finished something in eighty years. She is making a dress. She has been making this particular dress since the mid-twentieth century. The party it was designed for happened without her. She did not go.

She is a renowned designer. She was. She has not released a new design in eighty years, and nearly all of her former admirers have since died of old age.

---

## The Room
The tailoring room is dense with unfinished work — bolts of fabric in colors fashionable in other centuries, mannequins in half-placed garments, reference boards pinned with images she can no longer fully remember. The Kubler has crept into the room's corners. Francena does not appear to see it.

[Daniel Cadwallader](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s neck descends through a crack in the ceiling above the back wall. She is aware of it. She does not look at it.

---

## The Dress
Francena does not speak about the dress as a symbol. She measures. She pins. She adjusts a seam, decides it is wrong, starts that section again. The dress is almost right. It is always almost right.

Players who spend time with her learn, gradually, what the party was — and why she didn't go. The story is not dramatic. It is worse than dramatic: it was an ordinary choice. A small one. She does not know if it was wrong. She has had eighty years to not know.

## The Puzzle — Francena's Dress

| Solution | Method | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Ignore** | Leave without engaging | No consequence; no reward |
| **Trap** | Corner her against the wall so she cannot return to the dress | She distresses. Negative consequence for [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s arc; affects his ending scene |
| **The Perfect Dress** | Engage fully — help her work through the final design choices | She finishes it. Something shifts in the room. She joins the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md). [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md)'s arc moves toward release. |

## Notes
- The dress puzzle is Chapter 1's most optional and most time-consuming challenge. It requires no items — only patience and conversation.
- Francena's design studio was established as a condition of her relocation to the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md): [Daniel](dr-daniel-cadwallader.md) would not come without her, and she would not come without her workspace. The studio was built for her. She has been in it ever since.
