## Overview

Jone Ross ate a [god-seed](god-seeds.md) in 1866. He was nine years old. The exact circumstances are unclear; the seeds had been brought to the hotel by [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) as part of his ongoing arrangement with [Alfred Kubler](alfred-kubler.md), and one found its way to the boy. He died that same year, his heart stopped, and did not stay dead. [Barret Aricson](barret-aricson.md), trying to save him, gave him his own heart. It held for a year. When it stopped again, they tried something else. They found that organs taken from the [hotel's lost children](no-one-can-die.md) worked better. They have been using them ever since.

Jone is still dying. He has always been still dying. The god-seed keeps him from reaching the end of it, but it does not fix whatever is wrong. It simply postpones. Barret has spent 159 years postponing.

## What He Is Like

Jone is frozen at nine. Not just visually, but behaviourally, temporally, in the way he processes the world. He acts like a child because the last time he updated was 1866. His room is a child's room, preserved in perfect stillness. Entering it is, according to everyone who has done so, deeply unsettling in a way that takes a moment to name: everything is the right size for a nine-year-old, maintained as though he'll need it tomorrow, and he has needed it every tomorrow for a century and a half.

His only regular contact is with [Barret](barret-aricson.md), who treats him exactly like a child, protectively, with a directness that admits no ambiguity. They have been each other's entire world for most of recorded time.

## Symbolic Role

Jone mirrors [Custard Loevinger](custard-loevinger.md) structurally: a young boy, frozen, the reason an older figure has sacrificed everything. [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md)'s interactions with Jone are among the most emotionally loaded moments in the game. She does not know why. The game does not explain it to her. The player, by the time they reach Jone, likely understands before she does.

## In Gameplay

Jone appears in the endgame. Whether he gives [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) a key depends on what she has done throughout the game, specifically how she has treated the people around her and whether [Baya Ross](baya-ross.md) is still alive. If Amy has been too damaged by the [Sin System](sin-system.md), the encounter becomes a fight instead of an exchange.
