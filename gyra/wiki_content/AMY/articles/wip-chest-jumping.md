# WIP] Chest Jumping

Amy can also hide in chests distributed throughout the hotel floors to avoid being found by Barry. These chests have two entrances, allowing Amy to hide behind either of the doors. If the door she's hiding behind starts to vibrate because Barry is about to open it, she can quickly move to the other side to avoid being discovered.
