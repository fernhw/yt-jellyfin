[MAIN_SECTION]
Amy’s Escape has four very unique ways of handling difficulty.

Instead of the usual Easy, Medium, and Hard, we opted for a system where difficulty changes the game itself, not just numbers.

One difficulty makes the stealth harder. Characters become smarter, items may be removed, routes may change, and enemies can even leave their usual areas or combine forces against you.

Another difficulty focuses on puzzles. It does not simply make answers less obvious; it changes the puzzles into more complex versions, secretly dynamic enough to make familiar rooms feel wrong again.

The goal is to keep every mode fresh, uncomfortable, and worth replaying.
