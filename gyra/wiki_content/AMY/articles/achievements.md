## Why These Achievements Are Not Normal {#why}

Most games give you an achievement for doing the right thing. For finding everything. For being good. *Amy's Escape* does not.

**Every achievement is awarded for the wrong choice.**

Not because we want to reward cruelty. Because the wrong choice is the interesting one to document. The good solution to every puzzle is its own reward — the bar fills, the character heals, you get the ending that earns *"Thanks for playing."* That is the reward. An achievement on top of it would cheapen it. The game does not pop a notification when Jenna's birthday finally has enough guests. It just fills the bar. That's the whole thing.

The bad path gets the achievement icon because the bad path is what the game needs you to acknowledge. You chose it. You knew. The game will give you the achievement. It will not congratulate you.

---

## The Sin System

When Amy takes the wrong path on a character puzzle, the [Sin System](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) records it on her body. Literally. The marks are visible, persistent, and specific — soot from Sam's burning lamp, other textures from other choices. They last the rest of the run. They are not a punishment mechanic. They are a record.

The Sin System and the achievement system are the same system. Every Sin Mark is an achievement trigger. Every achievement icon is a Sin Mark on Amy's skin. By the time you've collected all of them, you can read the full history of what you chose off her.

---

## The Genocide Run Relationship

The [Genocide Run](the-genocide-run-ending.md) is the only way to collect every achievement in a single playthrough. It is built for this. The game is aware of what you are doing, and the puzzles get harder as you proceed.

You will get every achievement. Except one.

The **Fishbowl** ([Ending D](ending-d.md)) is locked out of the genocide run by design. [Evangeline](evangeline-aerona-and-ilina.md) will not appear for someone on that path. The ending about choosing to forget everything is reserved for Amy at her most broken, not Amy at her most cruel. The game distinguishes between the two. This is the only achievement the genocide run cannot give you, and the reason it can't is the thesis of the whole design.

---

## "Thanks for Playing"

After [Ending F](ending-f.md)'s credits roll, the screen reads **"Thanks for playing."** There is no pop. There is no icon during play. That is the achievement. If you got there, you know.
