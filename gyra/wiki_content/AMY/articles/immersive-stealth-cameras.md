# Immersive stealth cameras:

The game's visual mechanics are designed to heighten immersion by offering players a dual perspective. When Amy hides, the scene doesn't simply change to signify concealment. Players feel her hiding spot. If she ducks inside a drawer, they see the drawer doors close upon their viewpoint, plunging them into an encapsulating darkness. Similarly, while hiding within a clothes rack, the edges of the screen will be fringed with garments, mimicking the sensation of being swathed in fabric. This dual perspective is further exemplified when Amy interacts with objects. As she holds an item in third person, players simultaneously view a 'phantom' first-person hand, presenting the tactile sensation of that object as if they were Amy.
