## The ARG Structure

The ARG is [Melina](melinas-impossible-puzzle-arg.md)'s side quest. She was inside the [Ross Hotel](ross-hotel.md) for a very long time with no one to talk to and no way out. She used that time.

She left clues. She hid things. She documented what she knew about [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md), his departure, his letters, his last communication with [Barret](barret-aricson.md), and the reason he gave for leaving. She toyed with how the information was hidden — the ARG is not a straight document drop. It is a puzzle built by someone who had decades to make it interesting.

The web presence uses a 90s aesthetic: fake GeoCities styling, period-appropriate page design. The look is part of the fiction. Melina had a computer. The record looks like what she had to work with.

The ARG is complex by design. It rewards people who go all the way in. The truth about what happened to Ross after 1978 — his descent, his decision, what he wrote and what he didn't — lives inside it.
