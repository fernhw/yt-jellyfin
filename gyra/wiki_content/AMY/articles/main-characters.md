# Main characters

Amelia “Amy" Loevinger, a sweet maid at the Albert Ross Hotel, is responsible for maintaining the cleanliness of hallways and select rooms. She is not permitted to enter special guests' rooms, the kitchen, basement, or attic due to recent safety concerns.
Darby (glowing bird): A firebird from ancient times, Darby is a nocturnal creature that emits light to see in dark environments. While these birds are often associated with guiding weary travelers, they are more akin to sparkly cockatoos. Darby is well-trained.
Kole Wadjet, - A grumpy old man who has held numerous assistant positions throughout his life, now works as a receptionist and hotel manager's assistant at the Ross Hotel. He appears to never sleep, suggesting that some people may not have time for dreams.
Dr. Daniel Cadwallader, a highly renowned doctor hired to treat Baya Ross' severe illness, Dr. Cadwallader initially refused to move to Albert Ross' household but eventually agreed after being offered financial incentives. He dislikes confined spaces and has been waiting for his wife to find the perfect dress for several decades.
Nettle Tenesea - A resident of the hotel's pipes, Nettle was released from her watery prison in the bathing room and now follows Amy around in search of a well. She harbors resentment toward Barry for trapping her.
Eila Bartlett - The protector of the sky garden, Eila spends his days caring for the plants as if they were his own children. His love for plants is so strong that he has become one himself.
Rëlbûk Dërflä - The self-proclaimed major of the city of quitters, is no longer human but a crow. You see him as a crow, but you can incite his original self. Whoever he was, he's not good at hiding his old name.
Elliot is the gatekeeper. Not much is known about him other than the fact that he never fails his paperwork, he's a he, and he's called a good father by his wife, whom no one has seen before. It's hard to know if Elliot even exists.
The Knots (the couple whose names have been forgotten) are an inseparable couple who enjoy their golden years separated from society in the caverns. Their isolation and frugal lifestyle have driven them mad, but it hasn't changed their love for crunchy stuff.
Baya Ross - Albert Ross' beloved wife, Baya, fell gravely ill after their marriage. Despite Albert's best efforts to hire top doctors and scientists to cure her, it was ultimately a miracle that saved her. She has since regained her weight and then some.
Barret Aricson the manager of the Ross Hotel and Albert Ross' best friend, covers his face with drapes due to a severe skin condition. He holds the keys to all the doors in the hotel and is the only one who knows the way upstairs. Once known as "sweet Barry," his current sour demeanor is a mystery.
Aaron Kane - is the one-man show. owner, author, writer, director, producer, and performer of the Theater of Dreams. With his showmanship and expert storytelling, he's the best show the theater has ever seen, and also the only show the theater has ever seen.
The Puppets - are manifestations of Kane, parts of his body spread into countless puppets living in constant showmanship from him in the Theater of Dreams. (see 1.3.5 for more)
