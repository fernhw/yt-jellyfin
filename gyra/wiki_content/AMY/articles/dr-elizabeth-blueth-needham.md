[SECONDARY]
## Overview
[Dr. Blueth](dr-elizabeth-blueth-needham.md) was one of the original scientists assembled by [Albert Ross](albert-ross.md) to study the [God Seeds](god-seeds.md) extracted from the [Ingersole Mines](ingersole-caverns.md). She witnessed the experiments fracture. She agreed to stay. She has been here a very long time, playing solitaire at a table where the cards no longer have any color.

---

## The Research
Blueth's assignment within the scientific cohort was entomology and biological countermeasures. When the Kubler organism began spreading through the hotel, she was tasked with stopping it.

**Attempt 1 — The [Moonlight Aphids](the-12-moonlight-aphids.md):** She engineered a species of bioluminescent, firefly-like insects to feed on the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) and suppress its growth. They didn't work. The aphids ignored the [Kubler](the-kubler.md) and wandered the hotel eating other insects instead. They became a permanent fixture, now the game's primary collectible, scattered through every area.

**Attempt 2 — The Firebirds ([Darby](darby-glowing-bird.md)):** She engineered a line of firebirds to hunt the aphids. These also failed to perform their function. She made the entire line sterile. She put the last surviving bird in a storage cage, forgot about it, and went back to solitaire.

Blueth considers both of these failures. She is correct about the science. She is wrong about their importance.

---

## The Insect Room
Blueth has adapted her room into a terrarium facility housing **543 individual insects** in jars, cases, and contained environments. Each one is catalogued. She knows all of them. The room is meticulous in the way that only very long loneliness produces.

---

## Meeting Amy — The Solitaire Cards
Blueth's door in Chapter 1 is covered in cobwebs. Behind it she is at her table, mid-game, polite but closed.

**Optional:** [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) can find two missing solitaire cards elsewhere in the hotel and return them to Blueth. Blueth looks at the now-complete deck, realizes it was *always* an unwinnable configuration, and kicks the entire spread off the table in quiet fury. This moment, small, private, and absurd, breaks something loose. She becomes warm. She tells [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) what [Sam Bernal](sam-bernal.md) fears most. She becomes an ally.

---

## The Aphid Chain (Later Chapters)
If [Amy](amelia-amy-loevinger.md) finds and releases all twelve **[Jarred Male Aphids](jarred-male-aphids.md)** scattered through the hotel, Blueth will acknowledge that her experiment has conclusively failed. She will give Amy the **Queen Aphid** with instructions to "take it for a walk." This unlocks:
- Full immunity to the [Kubler Shrub](kubler-shrubed.md) infection in the [Ingersole Mines](ingersole-caverns.md)
- Access to previously unreachable areas in the mines
- The location of the final [God Seed](god-seeds.md)
- Blueth's appearance in the [Liminal Bar](the-liminal-bar.md) (her unlock is tied to the Aphid chain, not the standard puzzle system)

## Notes
- Blueth's long solitude has given her an unusual relationship with failure: she documents it meticulously and seems to have made peace with it, yet the solitaire cards reveal that the peace is not total.
- She was fully aware of what the experiments in the mines were doing. She stayed by choice. She has had a long time to decide how to feel about that.
