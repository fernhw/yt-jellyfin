All The Subscriptions I Don't Pay For

A list of services I would otherwise be subscribing to — and what's quietly doing the job for me from a Mac mini in the closet. Money totals at the very end.

Streaming & Movies

Netflix + HBO Max + Crunchyroll (the realistic combo)
$54.97/mo

Movies on Friday night, the new HBO drama everyone's talking about, and every anime simulcast the same hour Japan gets it — without juggling three apps, three logins, three bills, and content that disappears the month I want to rewatch it.

What runs instead → Jellyfin on the Mac mini, fed by an automated request portal — I (or family) search a title, click "request", and a few hours later it shows up in the library forever.

Music

Spotify Premium
$11.99/mo

Any song, any album, any time, no ads, offline, on every device in the house — including the obscure b-sides and game soundtracks Spotify doesn't even carry.

What runs instead → A personal music library on Jellyfin, auto-grown from Spotify links and a game-soundtrack scraper, played through Finamp on the phone.

YouTube Without Ads

YouTube Premium
$13.99/mo

Watch any YouTube channel ad-free, with background play on the phone, downloaded for offline.

What runs instead → An automated YouTube downloader that grabs subscribed channels into Jellyfin, with chapters, subs, and zero ads.

Audiobooks

Audible Premium Plus
$14.95/mo

A growing audiobook library to fall asleep to and listen to on walks, with bookmarks, speed control, and chapter sync across devices.

What runs instead → AudioBookshelf on the Mac, with progress sync to phone and a library I actually own.

Ebooks

Kindle Unlimited
$11.99/mo

Read whatever I want without paying $15 per book.

What runs instead → A personal ebook library hosted on the Mac, accessible from phone and tablet.

Manga

Shonen Jump + MangaPlus Pro
$4.99/mo

Read the latest chapters of every manga I follow, no ads, no daily-chapter caps, full back-catalog.

What runs instead → A manga downloader that pulls chapters into a personal library readable on phone or tablet.

Cloud Sync (Files Across Devices)

iCloud+ 2TB
$9.99/mo

A multi-TB pool where my documents and projects sync between phone, laptop, and home — without Apple or Google reading them.

What runs instead → Syncthing on the Mac mini and every device, with a real local disk as the source of truth.

Off-Site Backup

Backblaze Personal Backup
$9.99/mo

If the Mac mini gets stolen or the disk fails, I don't lose 10 years of stuff.

What runs instead → Automated rsync + Syncthing replication to off-site storage — full backup, no per-file fees.

Photo Library

Google Photos / iCloud Photos 2TB
$9.99/mo

Every photo from the phone backed up, searchable, viewable from anywhere — without handing my whole life to Google.

What runs instead → Phone camera roll auto-syncs into a personal photo library on the Mac via Syncthing.

Personal Assistant (24/7)

Part-time virtual assistant (executive assistant on retainer)
$400.00/mo

Someone available around the clock who reads my messages, schedules my day, reminds me of what's pending, parses my receipts, plans my week, books things, drafts replies, and remembers everything I've ever told them. A real human VA runs $800–1500/mo part-time; I'm budgeting half-rate because Agnos works 24/7 but isn't a human.

What runs instead → Agnos — my personal assistant, reachable on WhatsApp / Telegram / Discord, with persistent memory of who I am and what I'm working on. Every tool below is one of its jobs.

Auto-Diary

Day One Premium
$2.92/mo

A journal that captures what I did today, what I dreamed about, what's on my mind — without me having to sit down and write it.

What runs instead → The Diary Tracker tool managed by Agnos — writes my day automatically based on our conversations.

Daily Life Recap

Notion AI / Reflect
$10.00/mo

A clean nightly summary of what I did, what's pending, what's worth remembering — surfacing patterns I'd otherwise miss.

What runs instead → The Midnight Report tool managed by Agnos — runs every night, emails me the recap.

What runs instead → The Nutrition Tracker tool managed by Agnos. I type "ate 2 eggs and rice" on WhatsApp; it parses, looks up macros, logs them, tells me where I'm at for the day. Zero friction.
