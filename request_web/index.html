Track a Show
Step 1 of 8 — Display name
What's the show called?
This is the name shown in the app — e.g. Re Zero, The Boys
Step 2 of 8 — Torrent search term
How should we search for it?
Lowercase, no punctuation. Use the plain English name the torrent sites use.
→ re zero (not Re:Zero), the boys
Step 3 of 8 — Jellyfin folder
Which folder in Jellyfin Shows?
Exact folder name inside your Shows library — e.g. Re Zero.
Must already exist (or be created) in Jellyfin.
Step 4 of 8 — Content type
Anime or live-action?
Anime searches Nyaa.si first. Live-action searches The Pirate Bay and RARBG.
Step 5 of 8 — Season & episodes
Which season, and how many episodes?
Next episode = the first episode to download (usually 1 for a new season).
Total episodes = how many episodes in the season. Not sure? Add more — the system handles extras gracefully.
Step 6 of 8 — Release day
Which day does it air?
New episodes drop on this day. The scheduler searches within a few days of this date to catch delayed uploads.
Step 7 of 8 — Season status
Is this season currently airing?
If the season is mid-run and you want to catch up on past episodes immediately, turn on Download backlog now — it will queue every episode from 1 up to your "next episode" without waiting for release days.
